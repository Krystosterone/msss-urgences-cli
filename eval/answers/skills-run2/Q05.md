**No. Not finding a clinic appointment tonight doesn't by itself mean you need the ER; whether you need the ER depends on your symptoms, not on what clinics have open.** If your ear pain comes with warning signs (a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, confusion, or pain that is getting much worse), call 911 or go to the nearest ER. If you're not sure how serious it is, call Info-Santé 811: a nurse answers 24/7 and can tell you whether it can wait for a clinic or should be seen tonight.

**What the MSSS clinic data can and can't add:**

- It can't show tonight at all. MSSS publishes its clinic file weekly, and the newest date in it is 2026-10-28, so it never says what's open now.
- It does show that late appointments are rare around Ahuntsic. Over the seven dates I checked (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28), District Médical's physicians were the only group in the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord offering any appointments scheduled in the 21h–6h band, and only a few each day (3 on 2026-10-28). So an empty evening is normal there; it isn't a sign that something is wrong.
- Daytime offers are much more common. On Friday 2026-10-23, the same weekday as tomorrow, District Médical offered 268 physician appointments (21 of them scheduled before 9h), District Médical Junior 84, Clinique médicale Omnisanté 89 and Clinique médicale Désy 47. These were offered last month and are not openings for tomorrow, but they show which clinics usually have daytime appointments.

**Routes for tonight and tomorrow (if nothing above applies):**

- **Info-Santé 811**, any time, for a nurse's advice.
- **A pharmacist**, who can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Tomorrow:** your own family doctor or GMF if you have one; otherwise the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811. To find an actual slot, use Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.

The counts include only clinics connected to the provincial booking hub, and offered doesn't mean free: some slots were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients.
