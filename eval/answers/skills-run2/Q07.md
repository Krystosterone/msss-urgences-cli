This year's numbers so far can't be compared cleanly, because four Montréal facilities aren't in the 2026-2027 data yet.**

Each year below covers the same window, April 1 → end of period 7 (roughly mid-October), for the Montréal region as a whole:

- 2021-2022, 21 facilities: 25.2% of stretcher patients stayed 24 h or more; average ER stay 10.2 h (18.0 h for stretcher patients); 2.6 h on average from triage to a physician; 449,894 visits.
- 2022-2023, 21 facilities: 27.3% of stretcher patients stayed 24 h or more; average ER stay 11.0 h (19.9 h for stretcher patients); 2.8 h on average from triage to a physician; 470,449 visits.
- 2023-2024, 21 facilities: 26.7% of stretcher patients stayed 24 h or more; average ER stay 10.9 h (19.2 h for stretcher patients); 2.7 h on average from triage to a physician; 458,360 visits.
- 2024-2025, 21 facilities: 25.9% of stretcher patients stayed 24 h or more; average ER stay 10.7 h (18.7 h for stretcher patients); 2.8 h on average from triage to a physician; 506,551 visits.
- 2025-2026, 21 facilities: 24.6% of stretcher patients stayed 24 h or more; average ER stay 10.5 h (18.4 h for stretcher patients); 2.6 h on average from triage to a physician; 489,669 visits.
- 2026-2027, 17 facilities: 23.9% of stretcher patients stayed 24 h or more; average ER stay 11.0 h (19.0 h for stretcher patients); 2.7 h on average from triage to a physician; 425,586 visits.

My reading of the five comparable years (2021-2022 to 2025-2026, each with the same 21 facilities):

- **Long stretcher stays**, the clearest overcrowding signal, rose from 25.2% to a peak of 27.3% in 2022-2023, then fell each year to 24.6% in 2025-2026: slightly better than at the start.
- **Average stays** followed the same arc: up in 2022-2023, then down each year, though still a little longer in 2025-2026 than in 2021-2022.
- **The delay to see a physician** showed no clear direction: between 2.6 h and 2.8 h on average in every comparable year.
- **Volume grew**: more visits than in 2021-2022 by the same point of the year, for broadly similar performance. Visits are volume, not quality.

**Why 2026-2027 isn't comparable yet:** the region's total this year covers 17 facilities, 4 fewer than last year (facility count change −4). The live file's join with this cumulative file finds no 2026-2027 row for Hôpital du Sacré-Cœur, Hôpital Fleury, Hôpital Jean-Talon and Albert-Prévost, so their visits and stays are missing from this year's Montréal total. On its face, the average stay is 0.55 h longer than last year and the 24 h+ share 0.7 points lower, but with a different set of hospitals, neither is a real trend.

For context, Montréal runs above the province on long stretcher stays: 24.6% against 20.3% province-wide in 2025-2026.

These are averages over months of past visits. They describe how the region's ERs have performed, not how long a visit today would take.
