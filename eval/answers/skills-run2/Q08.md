**Orthopedic surgery waitlists have shrunk over the past two years, in Montréal and across Québec, and a smaller share of patients now waits more than 6 months. Montréal still has a somewhat larger share of long waits than the province as a whole.**

I compared four end-of-period snapshots: period 4 of each of the last three fiscal years, plus the period just before the latest one.

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery:**

- 2425-P04 (fiscal 2024-2025): 8,437 waiting; 2,914 over 6 months (35%); 685 over a year (8%).
- 2526-P04 (fiscal 2025-2026): 7,810 waiting; 2,918 over 6 months (37%); 463 over a year (6%).
- 2627-P03 (fiscal 2026-2027): 6,052 waiting; 1,824 over 6 months (30%); 218 over a year (4%).
- 2627-P04 (latest published): 6,265 waiting; 1,891 over 6 months (30%); 226 over a year (4%).

**All of Québec (sum of the regions reported), orthopedic surgery:**

- 2425-P04 (fiscal 2024-2025): 38,763 waiting; 13,640 over 6 months (35%); 3,193 over a year (8%).
- 2526-P04 (fiscal 2025-2026): 34,513 waiting; 11,613 over 6 months (34%); 2,085 over a year (6%).
- 2627-P03 (fiscal 2026-2027): 28,530 waiting; 7,207 over 6 months (25%); 969 over a year (3%).
- 2627-P04 (latest published): 29,359 waiting; 7,900 over 6 months (27%); 968 over a year (3%).

What I read from it:

- **The long-wait share fell.** In Montréal, the share waiting over 6 months went from 35% and 37% at period 4 of the two previous years to 30% now; waits over a year fell from 8% to 4%. Province-wide, the over-6-month share went from 35% to 27%.
- **Montréal vs Québec:** in the latest period, 30% of Montréal's orthopedic patients have waited over 6 months, against 27% province-wide, so Montréal runs a little above the provincial share.
- **The latest period ticked up slightly** in both, from 2627-P03 to 2627-P04. One period (about 4 weeks) is noise, not a trend.
- **Coverage gap:** the 2026-2027 provincial figures exclude Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported in those periods. It was included in the earlier years, when it had 2,503 orthopedic patients waiting at 2526-P04, so part of the drop in the provincial count comes from the missing region. The percentages are the fairer comparison across years.

**Limits:** these are snapshots at the end of each fiscal period, not today's list; 2627-P04 is the latest period MSSS has published, so it describes the list a few months ago. Wait time is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate, so time spent getting a referral or a specialist appointment isn't included. They are regional totals, not per-hospital figures, and they don't predict any one person's wait: the surgeon's office is the authority on that. If you're worried about your health while waiting, contact your surgeon's office or Info-Santé 811.
