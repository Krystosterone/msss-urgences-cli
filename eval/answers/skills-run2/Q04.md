Since you describe it as non-urgent, here are the non-urgent routes. If the ear pain comes with a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, confusion, or you feel very unwell, call 911 or go to the nearest ER instead; if you're unsure, Info-Santé 811 can advise.

**Around Ahuntsic, some fifteen clinics and CLSCs on the provincial booking hub regularly offer appointments, several of them in the evening and some on weekends. This shows what they offered in late October, not what's free today.**

I looked at the seven most recent dates MSSS has published, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28 (a full week, weekend included), for the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord. On 2026-10-28, 21 clinic/professional groups there offered appointments. The ones that stand out (the first four offered physician appointments on every one of those seven days):

- **District Médical:** by far the largest, 426 physician appointments on 2026-10-28, including 25 scheduled between 18h and 21h. It offered evening slots on all seven dates, and also on the weekend (72 appointments on Saturday 2026-10-24, 56 on Sunday 2026-10-25).
- **District Médical Junior:** 143 physician appointments on 2026-10-28, 8 of them in the evening; also open on the weekend (60 on 2026-10-24, 45 on 2026-10-25).
- **Centre médical Mieux-Être, succursale Ahuntsic:** 172 physician appointments on 2026-10-28, all during the day.
- **CLSC Ahuntsic:** 22 physician and 38 nurse appointments on 2026-10-28, and physician appointments on the weekend too (10 on 2026-10-24, 20 on 2026-10-25).
- **Clinique médicale Désy:** 113 physician appointments on 2026-10-28 (11 in the evening), plus 46 with a nurse; it had no physician offers on the Saturday.

"Physician" covers physicians and residents; "nurse" covers nurses and specialized nurse practitioners. A larger count means a clinic offers more appointments through the hub, not that you are more likely to get one.

**How to actually get seen:**

- **A pharmacist** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe. A good first stop for ear pain.
- **Info-Santé 811** (a nurse, 24/7) can tell you whether it needs a doctor and how soon.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To book a real slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

**Limits:** these are appointments that clinics connected to the provincial booking hub offered for each date. Some were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients, and clinics outside the hub aren't counted at all. The newest date is 2026-10-28, so none of this says what's open today.
