**No. Finding no clinic appointments after 9 pm doesn't mean you need the ER. Late appointments are rare, and whether to go depends on your symptoms, not on clinic schedules.**

- Very few clinics around Ahuntsic offer appointments that late. On 2026-10-28, the latest date MSSS has published, District Médical was the only clinic in the RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord to offer any in the 21h–6h band: 3 physician appointments.
- Tonight: Info-Santé 811 has a nurse on the line around the clock who can tell you whether this can wait until tomorrow and where to go. A pharmacist can also advise on ear pain.
- Tomorrow: book through Rendez-vous santé Québec or your own clinic. Without a family doctor, the GAP (Québec.ca or 811) can direct you.
- Call 911 or go to the nearest ER if you develop a high fever with severe pain, swelling or redness behind the ear, sudden hearing loss, a stiff neck or confusion.

The clinic data is past offers, published weekly, not what's open tonight.
