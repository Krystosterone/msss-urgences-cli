**Orthopedic waitlists have shrunk over the last two years, in Montréal and province-wide, and far fewer people now wait over a year.**

Montréal, at period 4 of each fiscal year:

- 2024-2025: 8,437 waiting, 685 of them for over a year.
- 2025-2026: 7,810 waiting, 463 for over a year.
- 2026-2027: 6,265 waiting, 226 for over a year. 30% have waited over 6 months, against 37% a year earlier.

Québec: 29,359 orthopedic patients waiting at 2627-P04, 968 of them for over a year, against 34,513 and 2,085 at 2526-P04. The 2026-2027 provincial figure excludes Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported this period, so part of that drop is a coverage gap. Montréal's figures are comparable.

Limits: these are end-of-period snapshots, and 2627-P04 is the latest period MSSS has published. The wait starts at the surgeon's decision to operate, not at the first referral. They're regional counts, not a prediction for any one patient.
