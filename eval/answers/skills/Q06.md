**No one can tell you which Montréal ER will see you fastest, or how long you'd wait: the data can't predict it.** ERs see people by triage priority, so a sprained ankle waits behind more urgent cases, and MSSS warns that a low count of people waiting doesn't mean being seen sooner.

Before going: for a minor injury, MSSS itself recommends calling Info-Santé 811, getting a clinic appointment or asking a pharmacist. Go to an ER, or call 911, if you can't put any weight on the foot, it looks deformed, or it's numb or cold.

If you still want the current picture, these were the least crowded general ERs at the last snapshot:

- Hôpital Notre-Dame: 71% stretcher occupancy, 9 people waiting to see a physician.
- Hôpital de Verdun: 104%, 6 waiting.
- Hôpital Maisonneuve-Rosemont: 133%, 66 waiting.

The Montréal mean was 144%. Sacré-Coeur, Fleury and Jean-Talon weren't reporting. I've left out the pediatric, psychiatric and cardiology ERs, which serve specific patients.
