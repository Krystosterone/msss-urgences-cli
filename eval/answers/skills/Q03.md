**A 2-year-old with a high fever who is very drowsy needs to be seen now. If your child is hard to wake, has trouble breathing or has a seizure, call 911. Otherwise go to the nearest ER now, whichever that is.** Don't drive to a farther hospital because it looks less busy. If you're unsure how serious it is, Info-Santé 811 has a nurse on the line around the clock, but don't wait to go if you're worried.

The crowding numbers can't tell you where you'd be seen faster. ERs see children by triage priority, so a very drowsy child with a fever is assessed ahead of less urgent cases wherever you arrive. Tell the triage nurse about the drowsiness.

For context only, at the last snapshot Sainte-Justine had 125% stretcher occupancy and 39 children waiting to see a physician, and the Montreal Children's had 175% and 27 waiting. That isn't a reason to choose one over the other.
