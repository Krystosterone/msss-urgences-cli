**Around Ahuntsic, several clinics regularly offer non-urgent appointments, some of them in the evening. MSSS's data shows which clinics offer them, not which are free right now.**

- On 2026-10-28, the latest date MSSS has published, 21 clinic and professional groups in the RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord offered 1372 appointments. District Médical offered 426 physician appointments, Centre médical Mieux-Être (Ahuntsic) 172 and District Médical Junior 143.
- District Médical offered evening (18h–21h) physician appointments on each of the seven latest dates, 25 of them on 2026-10-28. Clinique médicale Désy offered 11 that evening and District Médical Junior 8.

How to get a consultation for ear pain:

- Book through Rendez-vous santé Québec, or call one of these clinics.
- Info-Santé 811: a nurse can advise you, around the clock.
- A pharmacist can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- Your own family doctor or GMF, if you have one. Without one, the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP) can direct you, online at Québec.ca or through 811.
- If you get a high fever, severe pain, swelling behind the ear or sudden hearing loss, go to an ER or call 911.

These counts are appointments offered a week ago by clinics on the provincial booking hub, not openings now, and clinics outside the hub aren't counted.
