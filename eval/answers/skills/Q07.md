**Over the five comparable years, Montréal's ER crowding hasn't steadily worsened: it peaked in 2022-2023 and has eased since, though stretcher stays remain long.** All figures cover April 1 → period 7 of each fiscal year (roughly mid-October), for the Montréal region:

- Average stay on a stretcher: 18.0 h in 2021-2022, 19.9 h in 2022-2023, then 18.4 h in 2025-2026.
- Share of stretcher patients staying 24 h or more: 25.2%, then 27.3%, then 24.6%.
- Visits rose from 449,894 to 489,669 over the same years, so the ERs see more people than in 2021-2022.
- This fiscal year reads 19.0 h and 23.9%, but it isn't comparable: it covers 17 Montréal ERs against 21 before, because there is no data yet this year for the four Nord-de-l'Île-de-Montréal hospitals (Sacré-Coeur, Fleury, Jean-Talon, Albert-Prévost).

The file doesn't go further back than 2021-2022, so it can't say how today compares with ten years ago.

_MSSS cumulative ER file (Données cumulatives des urgences), fiscal year 2026-2027 through period 7, with the five previous years._
