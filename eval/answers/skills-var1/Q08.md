**Orthopedic surgery waitlists are shorter now than at the same point in the two previous years, in Montréal and across Québec: fewer people waiting, and a smaller share waiting over 6 months, with most of the improvement in the past year. Montréal's long-wait share is now a little above the provincial one.**

I compared period 4 of each of the last three fiscal years, plus period 3 of this year.

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery:**
- Waiting over 6 months: 2,914 (35%) in 2425-P04, 2,918 (37%) in 2526-P04, 1,891 (30%) in 2627-P04.
- Waiting over a year: 685, then 463, then 226.
- Total waiting: 8,437, then 7,810, then 6,265.
- Latest period vs the one before (2627-P03): the total rose from 6,052 to 6,265 and those over 6 months from 1,824 to 1,891, with the share steady at 30%. A single period is noise, not a trend.

**Québec as a whole, orthopedic surgery:**
- Share waiting over 6 months: 35% in 2425-P04, 34% in 2526-P04, 27% in 2627-P04; over a year: 8%, 6%, 3%.
- Total waiting: 38,763, then 34,513, then 29,359 (7,900 of them over 6 months in 2627-P04).
- The 2026-2027 provincial figures exclude Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported in either 2026-2027 period, so part of the drop in provincial counts comes from that missing region. The percentages are the fairer comparison, and they also improved.

So Montréal's 30% over 6 months is a bit above the provincial 27%.

**Limits:** these are end-of-period snapshots (latest: period 4 of fiscal 2026-2027), not today's list. The wait is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate, so time spent getting a family doctor or a specialist appointment isn't included. They're regional totals, not per hospital, and they don't predict any one person's wait: the surgeon's office is the authority on that.

_MSSS surgical waitlist file (Chirurgies, portrait de la liste d'attente), periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04._
