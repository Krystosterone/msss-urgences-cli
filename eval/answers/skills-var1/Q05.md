**No. Finding no clinic appointment tonight after 9 pm doesn't mean you need the ER. Whether you need the ER is a medical question that appointment listings can't answer.**

- **Late-night clinic appointments are rare around Ahuntsic anyway.** In MSSS's clinic file for the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord, over the 7 most recent published dates (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28), District Médical was the only clinic that offered any physician appointments scheduled between 21h and 6h, and only a handful a day (between 1 and 6). An empty late evening is normal and says nothing about how urgent your problem is.
- **Most appointments are offered in the morning.** On Thursday 2026-10-22, District Médical alone offered 154 physician appointments between 9h and 12h. Those were past offers, not openings now: the file shows which clinics usually offer appointments and when, not what's free tonight or tomorrow.
- **If you're unsure whether it can wait until tomorrow,** call Info-Santé 811: a nurse will advise you by phone, 24/7. A pharmacist can also advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Tomorrow:** your own family doctor or GMF if you have one; without one, the GAP (Québec.ca or 811). To find an actual slot, use Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.
- **The reverse holds too:** an available appointment wouldn't mean you can skip the ER if things get worse. Go to the ER or call 911 if you develop warning signs: trouble breathing, chest pain, a high fever, severe or rapidly worsening pain, a stiff neck, confusion or unusual drowsiness, or (for an ear problem) swelling or redness behind the ear.

_MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file (appointments offered through the provincial booking hub), published weekly on Thursdays at 14h, up to the day before; the latest date available is 2026-10-28._
