**A 2-year-old who is very drowsy with a fever needs to be seen now: call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away. Please don't pick an ER by how busy it looks, and don't drive past a closer one.**

- Being very drowsy or hard to wake is one of the signs that can mean something serious in a child. If your child is hard to wake, has trouble breathing, has a seizure, or you're scared, call 911.
- The nearest ER is the right one. Montréal's two pediatric ERs are CHU Sainte-Justine and the Montreal Children's, but general ERs also see children in an emergency.
- ERs see children by how urgent their condition is, not in arrival order and not by how full the ER is. The crowding numbers can't tell you where your child would be seen sooner.
- Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, 24/7) is for when you're unsure how serious something is. With a very drowsy child, don't let a phone call delay going.

For context only, not a reason to choose one or to wait, here is what MSSS reports for the two pediatric ERs:

- **CHU Sainte-Justine:** 88% stretcher occupancy, 9 children waiting to see a physician.
- **Montreal Children's:** 183% stretcher occupancy, 21 children waiting to see a physician.

These are head counts at one moment, not wait times: they don't show staffing or how sick the other children are, and they can change quickly.
