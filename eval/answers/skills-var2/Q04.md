**Several clinics in the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord area regularly offer non-urgent appointments through the provincial booking hub, including some in the evening and on weekends.**

**Ways to get seen for ear pain**

- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems like ear pain and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7, who can also tell you where to consult.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811 (check Québec.ca for current eligibility).
- **To find an actual appointment:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

**Which clinics usually offer appointments** (MSSS clinic file, RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord; I looked at the seven most recent published dates, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28)

- **Offered physician appointments on every one of the seven dates:** District Médical, District Médical Junior, Centre médical Mieux-Être succ. Ahuntsic, CLSC Ahuntsic and Clinique médicale Omnisanté. On the latest date (Wednesday 2026-10-28), District Médical offered 426 physician appointments and Centre médical Mieux-Être succ. Ahuntsic 172.
- **Evenings (18h–21h):** District Médical offered physician appointments in that band on all seven dates (25 on 2026-10-28). Clinique médicale Désy (11) and District Médical Junior (8) also did on 2026-10-28, and on some of the other dates.
- **Weekends:** on Saturday 2026-10-24, 7 clinics offered physician appointments, and on Sunday 2026-10-25 also 7; District Médical, District Médical Junior, Centre médical Mieux-Être succ. Ahuntsic, CLSC Ahuntsic and Clinique médicale Omnisanté offered some on both days.
- **Nurse appointments** (Infirmière, nurses and specialized nurse practitioners) also appear, for example at CLSC Ahuntsic and Clinique médicale Désy on weekdays.
- Other clinics in the same RLS with physician offers on 2026-10-28 include Clinique médicale Désy, Clinique médicale Rayons De Soleil, Clinique médicale Urgence 241 Ahuntsic, CLSC de Montréal-Nord and District Médical St-Sulpice. Some are in Montréal-Nord, so check which is closest to you.

**What this can't tell you:** these are appointments that were *offered* on past dates, not openings today. Some were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients, and clinics outside the booking hub aren't counted. MSSS republishes the file weekly (Thursdays at 14h), so the newest date is 2026-10-28.

**When it's no longer "nothing urgent":** if the ear pain comes with a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck or severe headache, sudden hearing loss, or weakness of the face, call 811 for advice or go to an ER; call 911 if it seems serious.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file (appointments offered through the provincial booking hub), published weekly; data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
