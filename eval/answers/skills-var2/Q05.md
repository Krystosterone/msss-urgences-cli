**No. Not finding a clinic appointment tonight doesn't mean you need the ER. That depends on your symptoms, not on whether a slot is listed.**

- **If it's still the non-urgent ear pain you described**, you have options tonight: Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, 24/7) can tell you whether you should be seen tonight or can wait for an appointment, and where; a pharmacist can advise on ear pain and, for some conditions, prescribe. For an appointment tomorrow, try your own clinic or GMF, Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the GAP (Québec.ca or 811) if you have no family doctor.
- **Late-evening slots are rare anyway.** In MSSS's clinic file for the RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord, over the seven most recent published dates (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28), only District Médical offered any physician appointments in the 21h–6h band, a handful per date (from 1 on 2026-10-26 to 6 on 2026-10-24). Most offers are in the morning and afternoon. So an empty late-evening list is normal and says nothing about how serious your problem is.
- That file shows past offers (published weekly; latest date 2026-10-28), not what is open tonight, and it only counts clinics on the provincial booking hub.

**Go to the ER, or call 911 if it seems serious,** if the ear pain comes with a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck or severe headache, confusion, sudden hearing loss, or weakness of the face. When you're unsure, call 811 first.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file, published weekly; data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
