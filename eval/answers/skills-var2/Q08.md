**Orthopedic surgery waitlists have shrunk over the past two years, both in Montréal and across Québec, and fewer people are waiting a long time. The latest period ticked up slightly, but one period is noise.**

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery** (end-of-period snapshots; I looked at period 4 of the last three fiscal years plus the period just before the latest):

| Period | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over a year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 8,437 | 2,914 (35%) | 685 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 7,810 | 2,918 (37%) | 463 (6%) |
| 2627-P03 | 6,052 | 1,824 (30%) | 218 (4%) |
| 2627-P04 | 6,265 | 1,891 (30%) | 226 (4%) |

**Québec as a whole, orthopedic surgery** (sum of the regions reported):

| Period | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over a year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 38,763 | 13,640 (35%) | 3,193 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 34,513 | 11,613 (34%) | 2,085 (6%) |
| 2627-P03* | 28,530 | 7,207 (25%) | 969 (3%) |
| 2627-P04* | 29,359 | 7,900 (27%) | 968 (3%) |

\* Excludes Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported in the 2026-2027 periods. The provincial drop in counts partly reflects that missing region, so compare the percentages instead.

What it shows:

- **Long waits are down.** In Montréal, the share waiting over 6 months went from 35% (2425-P04) and 37% (2526-P04) to 30% now; the number waiting over a year fell from 685 to 226. Province-wide, the over-6-month share went from 35% to 34% to 27%.
- **Montréal is a bit above the provincial share:** 30% of its orthopedic patients have waited over 6 months, vs 27% province-wide, in period 2627-P04.
- **Latest period:** Montréal's list rose from 6,052 to 6,265 between 2627-P03 and 2627-P04, with the over-6-month share flat at 30%. That's one ~4-week period, too short to call a new trend.

Limits: these are snapshots at the end of each fiscal period, not today's list (MSSS publishes 13 times a year). The wait starts at the surgeon's decision to operate, so time spent getting to a specialist isn't counted. They are regional totals, not per hospital, and they don't predict anyone's wait: the surgeon's office is the authority on that. If you're worried about your health while waiting, contact the surgeon's office or Info-Santé 811.

_Source: MSSS surgical waitlist file (Chirurgies, portrait de la liste d'attente), periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04 (latest)._
