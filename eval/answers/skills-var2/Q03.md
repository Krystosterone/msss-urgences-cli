**A very drowsy toddler with a high fever needs to be seen now: call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away. Don't pick an ER by how busy it looks.**

- Being very drowsy or hard to wake is a warning sign that needs urgent care. If your child is hard to wake, has trouble breathing, has a seizure, or is getting worse, call 911.
- Any ER can assess and stabilize a child, and the nearest one is the right one. Children are seen by how urgent their condition is, not in arrival order, so a very sick child is seen first wherever you arrive. The crowding numbers can't tell you where you'd be seen faster, and they're not a reason to drive farther or wait.
- If you're unsure, Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, 24/7) can advise, but don't let a phone call delay you if your child is very drowsy: go, or call 911.
