**Please don't choose based on which ER is less busy. A toddler with a high fever who is very drowsy should be seen right away. Go to the nearest pediatric ER now.** That's Sainte-Justine (3175 Côte-Sainte-Catherine) or the Montreal Children's Hospital (Glen site, 1001 Décarie), whichever is closer to you.
- **Call 911** if your child is hard to wake or doesn't respond normally, has trouble breathing, has a seizure, has a purple/red rash that doesn't fade when pressed, has a stiff neck, or seems to be getting worse.
- Drowsiness that doesn't improve once the fever comes down is a warning sign. When you arrive, tell the triage nurse clearly that your child is "very drowsy / hard to wake". A drowsy child with a fever is triaged as a priority, so the waiting-room crowding matters much less for you.
- If you're unsure and your child is easy to wake and responding normally, you can call **Info-Santé 811, option 1 then option 2** (pediatric line, 24/7) right now for a nurse's assessment. Don't delay going if you're worried.

About "less busy": I couldn't access live data for either ER tonight, so I can't tell you which one is less crowded. Even if I could, the crowding numbers mostly reflect patients on stretchers, and your child's triage level will determine how fast you're seen.
