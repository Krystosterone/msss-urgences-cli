I also have no hour-by-hour history for Sainte-Justine, so I can't say what's "normal" for a Wednesday evening. Here's some general context:
- In past years, pediatric ER occupancy in Montréal has spiked during respiratory-virus season. Reported peaks were around 140–175% at Sainte-Justine. The season usually starts building in November.
- One independent tracker of Québec ER data found that, across all ERs, Tuesdays and Wednesdays and the afternoon/evening hours tend to be the busiest. It covered a single period in an unspecified year.

For a current reading, check quebec.ca/SituationUrgences or Index Santé (indexsante.ca/urgences). Both show the occupancy rate and the number of people waiting, updated about hourly. If your child needs care, don't let the crowding level decide whether you go. Call Info-Santé 811 (option 1, then 2 for the pediatric line) if you're unsure.
