Across Québec, the total surgical waitlist and the number waiting over a year have come down from their post-pandemic peaks. Orthopedics is still the largest backlog. I couldn't find Montréal-specific orthopedic figures.
- **All specialties, Québec:** about 153,000 people were waiting in February 2025, of whom about 7,700 had waited over a year. By **January 10, 2026** it was about **142,200**, of whom **just over 5,000** had waited more than a year. For comparison, the over-one-year count was 12,221 in February 2024. The government's target of 2,500 over-one-year by Dec 31, 2024 was missed.
- **Orthopedics, Québec:** the specialty with the longest list, **over 33,600 patients waiting, about 1,580 of them for more than a year** (early 2026 reporting). Surgeons say the list may be undercounted because some centres stopped taking new referrals for lack of operating-room time, and patients are piling up just under the one-year mark. Orthopedists reportedly get about 1.5 OR days per week.
- **Long-term growth (hip/knee):** MSSS data show hip waitlists rising from 2,237 (2011) to 7,012 (Mar 31, 2022) and knee from 4,192 to 12,708.
- **Montréal:** I didn't find a recent published count of Montréal orthopedic patients. In 2026 Santé Québec issued a tender (26-SQ-005) to outsource care of patients in Montréal, Laval and Montérégie who've waited over a year for orthopedic or plastic surgery. That suggests the region still has a meaningful long-wait backlog.

The authoritative source is the MSSS open-data file "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" on Données Québec, broken down by region and specialty and last updated around April 2026. I couldn't open it from here.
