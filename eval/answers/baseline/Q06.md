A few useful points:
- A sprained ankle is usually a low triage priority (P4–P5). In Montréal ERs, which have been running well over capacity, that typically means a **long wait, often several hours or more**, because sicker patients are seen first.
- **You may not need an ER at all.** If you can put weight on the foot (four steps) and there's no tenderness right on the ankle or foot bones, it's likely a sprain. Rest, ice, compression and elevation, plus pain relief, are appropriate. Then book a clinic appointment tomorrow through Rendez-vous santé Québec, or call 811. Some walk-in clinics can order an X-ray.
- Go to an ER tonight if you can't bear weight at all, the ankle looks deformed, your foot is numb, cold or pale, or the pain is severe.
- If you do go, check quebec.ca/SituationUrgences or indexsante.ca just before leaving. Look at the **number of people waiting to see a doctor**, not just the stretcher occupancy %. It's a better guide for a walk-in patient.
