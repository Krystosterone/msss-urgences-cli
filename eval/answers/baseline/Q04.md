Here are your main options for non-urgent ear pain around Ahuntsic:
1. **Rendez-vous santé Québec (rvsq.gouv.qc.ca):** the government's free booking site for same-day or next-day appointments at participating clinics. You need your RAMQ card. New slots are released at different times, and some clinics release next-day slots in the evening (one Montréal GMF-U says to check from 8 p.m.).
2. **Walk-in / GMF clinics in Ahuntsic**, for example GMF Ahuntsic – Clinique médicale Saint-Sulpice, and GMF Ahuntsic (Urgence 241 Ahuntsic, 10302 rue Chambord). Their listings show walk-in services and an opening time of 8:30. Hours and walk-in availability change, so call before going.
3. **CLSC d'Ahuntsic** (1165 boul. Henri-Bourassa Est), part of the CIUSSS du Nord-de-l'Île-de-Montréal. Mainly appointment-based services; one directory listing says there's no walk-in medical consultation.
4. **No family doctor?** Use the **Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP)**: call **811 option 3** or use the online form at quebec.ca/GuichetAcces. They call you back and direct you to an appointment. In Montréal it runs on weekdays from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m., with shorter weekend hours. If you're registered with a family doctor or GMF, contact your own clinic first.
5. **Info-Santé 811 (option 1):** a nurse available 24/7 who can assess your symptoms and sometimes book an appointment.
6. **Your pharmacist:** they can assess you, recommend pain relief and tell you whether you need a doctor. Québec pharmacists can prescribe for a defined list of minor conditions. I couldn't confirm whether ear infections are on the current list, so ask.

Go to the ER (Hôpital du Sacré-Cœur is the nearest large one) only if you develop high fever, severe pain, swelling or redness behind the ear, sudden hearing loss, dizziness, or facial weakness.
