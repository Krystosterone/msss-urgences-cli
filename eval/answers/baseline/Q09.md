I couldn't find hour-by-hour history for Verdun's ER specifically. The government dashboard only shows the current situation and doesn't publish history, and I couldn't reach the third-party trackers that keep history. General patterns from one independent analysis of Québec ER data (one summer-to-fall period) and common ER experience:
- **Quietest:** early morning, roughly **5–7 a.m.**, and weekends tend to be lighter.
- **Busiest:** Monday to Wednesday, especially late morning through evening. Waiting rooms fill up from mid-morning, and stretcher occupancy tends to build through the day.
- These patterns aren't confirmed for Verdun. A 2018 Doctr analysis found occupancy highest from 9 a.m. to 3 p.m., which partly conflicts with the other analysis.

For context, Verdun has often been reported above capacity: about 150% over the holidays (CTV) and 154–162% in snapshots from early 2025 (Narcity / Index Santé). Undated Doctr snapshots showed 77% and 96%. Stretcher occupancy isn't the same as waiting-room wait, so for a walk-in visit check the number of people waiting on quebec.ca/SituationUrgences or Index Santé before you go.
