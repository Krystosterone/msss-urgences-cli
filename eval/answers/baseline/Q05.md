No. For non-urgent ear pain, having no clinic appointments tonight is not a reason to go to the ER. Very few clinics offer appointments after 9 p.m. In Montréal's busy ERs, a non-urgent problem like this would likely mean a very long wait, often many hours, behind sicker patients. I couldn't access tonight's live ER figures to give you a number.

What I'd suggest instead:
- **Tonight:** take over-the-counter pain relief (acetaminophen or ibuprofen if they're safe for you) and call **Info-Santé 811 (option 1)**. A nurse is available 24/7, can tell you whether this can wait until tomorrow, and can sometimes arrange an appointment.
- **Tomorrow morning:** check **Rendez-vous santé Québec** again. New slots get released, including next-day slots in the evening or early morning. Also try the walk-in at GMF Ahuntsic (opens around 8:30) or your own family doctor or GMF. If you don't have a family doctor, call **811 option 3 (GAP)** from 8 a.m.
- **Pharmacist:** a pharmacist can advise you tonight if a pharmacy is open late, or first thing tomorrow.

**Go to the ER (or call 911)** if you get a high fever with severe pain, swelling or redness behind the ear, sudden hearing loss, severe dizziness, facial weakness, a stiff neck or confusion.
