**For non-urgent ear pain you have several routes, and plenty of clinics in your area regularly offer appointments through the provincial booking system, including some in the evening.**

**Ways to get seen (MSSS's recommended routes)**
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7, including whether and where you should be seen.
- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe. Ask them whether your ear pain is something they can assess.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811, can direct you to a clinic. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To find an actual slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

**Which clinics around Ahuntsic usually offer appointments** (MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" data, RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord)
- Over the window 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, MSSS has data for all **7 dates**, and **15 clinics** in the RLS offered appointments on at least one of them.
- Five clinics offered physician appointments on every one of those dates: Centre médical Mieux-Être (succ. Ahuntsic), CLSC Ahuntsic, Clinique médicale Omnisanté, District Médical and District Médical Junior.
- On the latest date, 2026-10-28 (a Wednesday), District Médical offered **426 physician appointments**, **25 of them between 18h and 21h**; Centre médical Mieux-Être offered **172 physician appointments**; District Médical Junior **143 physician appointments** (**8 between 18h and 21h**); Clinique médicale Désy **113 physician appointments** (**11 between 18h and 21h**) plus **46 nurse appointments**; CLSC Ahuntsic **38 nurse appointments**.
- Evening (18h–21h) physician slots showed up regularly at District Médical, District Médical Junior, Clinique médicale Désy and the CLSC de Montréal-Nord. That means they *do* evening appointments, not that they're open or have room tonight.

**Limits of this data:** these are appointments clinics *offered* for past dates, not openings now. Some were booked, some are reserved for the clinic's own patients, and clinics outside the booking hub aren't counted. The file is published weekly (Thursdays at 14h, so a new one is due today), and its newest date is about a week old. More offers just means a clinic puts more appointments through the hub, not better odds for you. Montréal-Nord clinics are in the same RLS; one in a neighbouring RLS may be closer to you.

**When it's no longer "nothing urgent":** a high fever, severe or worsening pain, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, confusion, or sudden hearing loss or dizziness. Then call 811 for advice, or 911 / the nearest ER if it seems serious.

_Source: MSSS "Portrait quotidien des rendez-vous en clinique", published weekly; data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28 (latest date 2026-10-28)._
