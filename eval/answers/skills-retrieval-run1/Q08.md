**Orthopedic waitlists have shrunk over the last three years, both in Montréal and across Québec, and the share of people waiting more than 6 months has dropped clearly. Montréal still runs a little above the provincial share.**

**Same period (period 4, ending in late July) in each of the last three fiscal years**

| Orthopedic surgery | 2425-P04 | 2526-P04 | 2627-P04 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Montréal**, people waiting | MTL 8,437 | MTL 7,810 | MTL 6,265 |
| Montréal, waiting over 6 months | MTL 2,914 (35%) | MTL 2,918 (37%) | MTL 1,891 (30%) |
| Montréal, waiting over a year | MTL 685 (8%) | MTL 463 (6%) | MTL 226 (4%) |
| **Québec** (sum of reporting regions), share over 6 months | QC 35% | QC 34% | QC 27% |
| Québec, share over a year | QC 8% | QC 6% | QC 3% |

- **Montréal:** in 2627-P04, 4,374 people had been waiting under 6 months, 1,665 between 6 and 12 months and 226 over a year. The over-6-month share fell from 37% a year earlier to 30%, and the over-a-year count is about half its 2025-2026 level.
- **Province:** the long-wait shares fell the same way. I compare shares rather than counts across years because the latest period is missing a region (below).

**Coverage caveat for the provincial figures:** in 2627-P04, **Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec** isn't reported, so the provincial sum (**29,359 people**, **7,900** over 6 months) excludes it. The year before it was included and had **2,503** orthopedic patients waiting, so part of the provincial drop from **34,513** in 2526-P04 reflects the missing region, not shorter lists.

**Latest period-to-period move (one period is noise):** from 2627-P03 to 2627-P04, Montréal's orthopedic list went from **6,052** to **6,265** with the over-6-month share flat at **30% in both**; province-wide (Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec missing in both) it went from **28,530** to **29,359**, and the over-6-month share from **25%** to **27%**. That's a small uptick within one season, not a trend.

**Limits**
- These are end-of-period snapshots. 2627-P04 is the latest period MSSS has published, and it ends in late July, so it is more than three months old.
- The wait is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate. Time spent getting a family doctor or a specialist appointment isn't included.
- They're regional aggregates: not per hospital, and they don't predict any one person's wait. Your surgeon's office is the authority on that. If you're worried about your health while waiting, contact them or call 811.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" (Chirurgie_ListeAttente.csv), periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04._
