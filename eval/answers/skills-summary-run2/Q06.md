**The open data can't tell which ER will see you fastest or how long you'd wait, so I can't pick one for you.** For an estimated wait, Québec.ca's "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences) shows, for each ER, an estimated wait before seeing a physician for a non-priority case. It's an estimate that depends on the staff and patients present when you arrive, it doesn't apply to urgent cases, and it isn't a promise.

**For a sprain you may not need an ER at all.** Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, 24/7) can tell you where to go; a pharmacist can advise on minor injuries; your family doctor or GMF, or a clinic through Rendez-vous santé Québec, may see you. Without a family doctor, the GAP (Québec.ca or 811) can direct you. Go to an ER, or call 911, if you can't put any weight on the foot, the ankle looks deformed, the foot is numb, cold or turning blue, or there's an open wound.
