**Orthopedic waitlists have shrunk over the last two years, in Montréal and across Québec, and fewer people are waiting a long time. In period 4 of 2026-2027 (ending in late July 2026), 30% of Montréal's orthopedic patients had been waiting over 6 months, against 37% a year earlier.**

Same period (P04) in each of the last three fiscal years, so the season is the same:

| Orthopedic surgery | 2425-P04 | 2526-P04 | 2627-P04 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Montréal: waiting over 6 months | 35% | 37% | 30% |
| Montréal: waiting over a year | 685 people (8%) | 463 people (6%) | 226 people (4%) |
| Montréal: total waiting | 8,437 | 7,810 | 6,265 |
| Québec: waiting over 6 months | 35% | 34% | 27% |
| Québec: waiting over a year | 8% | 6% | 3% |
| Québec: total waiting | 38,763 | 34,513 | 29,359 |

- **Montréal now:** 6,265 people waiting for orthopedic surgery: 4,374 under 6 months, 1,665 between 6 and 12 months and 226 over a year. Its long-wait share runs a bit above the province's (30% against 27% over 6 months).
- **Province, with a caveat:** the 2627-P04 figures exclude Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported this period but was included in the two earlier years. So part of the drop in Québec's *count* is just the missing region; the percentages are the fairer comparison, and they fell too.
- **Most recent period:** from P03 to P04 Montréal's orthopedic list grew by 213 people (6,052 to 6,265), after shrinking by 296 the period before; province-wide (same regions in both periods) it grew by 829. One period (about four weeks) is noise; the three-year comparison above is the better guide.

Limits: these are end-of-period snapshots (period 4 ended in late July, so the data is over three months old), counted from the surgeon's decision to operate. Time spent getting a specialist appointment isn't included. They're regional totals, not per-hospital, and they don't predict anyone's own wait. If you're on a waitlist and worried about your health, your surgeon's office, or Info-Santé 811, is the place to ask.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" file, periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04._
