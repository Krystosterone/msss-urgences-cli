**For non-urgent ear pain you have several routes around Ahuntsic: a pharmacist, Info-Santé 811, your own family doctor or GMF, and the clinics of the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord network, several of which offered physician appointments every day last week.**

**Ways to get seen**

- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe. A good first stop to ask whether your ear pain needs a doctor.
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor:** the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811 (check Québec.ca for current eligibility).
- **To book an actual slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

**Which clinics nearby usually offer appointments** (RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, data for all 7 days)

- 15 clinics offered appointments through the provincial booking hub that week: physician appointments at 15 of them, nurse appointments at 9.
- 5 clinics offered physician appointments on every one of the 7 days: CENTRE MÉDICAL MIEUX-ÊTRE SUCC. AHUNTSIC, CLSC AHUNTSIC, Clinique médicale Omnisanté, District Médical and District Médical Junior (alphabetical, not a ranking).
- Evening physician appointments (scheduled 18h–21h) came from 9 clinics at least once; District Médical offered them on all 7 days and Clinique médicale Désy on 4 of 7.

These are appointments that were *offered* for those past dates, not openings now: some were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients, and clinics outside the booking hub aren't counted. Only the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord network was looked up; a clinic in a neighbouring network may be closer to you.

Since you describe it as not urgent, any of these routes is reasonable. Get care sooner (811 can help you decide) if the pain becomes severe, you have a high fever, there's swelling or redness behind the ear, fluid or blood coming from the ear, sudden hearing loss or dizziness. A stiff neck with fever, or confusion, means the ER or 911.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file, published weekly (Thursdays at 14h, up to the day before); data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, the latest published dates as of this morning._
