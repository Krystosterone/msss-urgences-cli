**No. Not finding a clinic appointment after 9 pm doesn't mean you need the ER, and it doesn't mean you don't. That depends on your symptoms, and the clinic data can't decide it.**

- **If your symptoms could be serious** (trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, being very drowsy or hard to wake), call 911 or go to the nearest ER now, whatever the clinic listings show.
- **If you're not sure how serious it is**, call Info-Santé 811: a nurse, 24/7, who can tell you whether it can wait for a clinic appointment or needs to be seen tonight.
- **For a minor problem**, other routes: a pharmacist (advice, and for some conditions a prescription), your own family doctor or GMF, the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP) through Québec.ca or 811 if you have no family doctor, and Rendez-vous santé Québec or a clinic's own booking system for a daytime or evening slot.

**What the clinic data says about late evenings near Ahuntsic.** MSSS's clinic file only shows appointments that were offered on past dates, not what's open tonight. In the latest week published (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28), appointments scheduled between 21h and 6h were rare in the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord network: of the 15 clinics with offers, only 1, District Médical, offered physician appointments in that band, 25 over the whole week (some on each of the 7 days; 2 on Thursday 2026-10-22). Evening slots between 18h and 21h were more common: 9 clinics offered physician appointments in that band at least once. So finding nothing listed late at night is what usually happens there; it says nothing about how urgent your situation is.

These counts include only clinics on the provincial booking hub, and an offered slot may already have been booked or reserved for a clinic's own patients.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file, published weekly; data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28 (the latest dates available this morning, not tonight)._
