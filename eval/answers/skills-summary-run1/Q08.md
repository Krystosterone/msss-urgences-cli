**Long waits for orthopedic surgery have shrunk, in Montréal and across Québec.** In the latest period, period 4 of fiscal year 2026-2027 (2627-P04), Montréal had 6,265 people waiting for orthopedic surgery, 30% for over 6 months (1,891 people) and 226 for over a year. Québec-wide (excluding Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec), 29,359 people were waiting, 27% of them for over 6 months, so Montréal's long-wait share runs a little above the province's.

**The trend, same period (P04) in each of the last 3 fiscal years** (waitlists move with the seasons, so this compares like with like):
- Montréal, share waiting over 6 months: 35% (2425-P04), 37% (2526-P04), 30% (2627-P04). Over a year: 8% (2425-P04), 6% (2526-P04), 4% (2627-P04).
- Montréal, people waiting: 8,437 (2425-P04), 7,810 (2526-P04), 6,265 (2627-P04); over 6 months: 2,914 (2425-P04), 2,918 (2526-P04), 1,891 (2627-P04); over a year: 685 (2425-P04), 463 (2526-P04), 226 (2627-P04).
- Québec, share waiting over 6 months: 35% (2425-P04), 34% (2526-P04), 27% (2627-P04). Over a year: 8% (2425-P04), 6% (2526-P04), 3% (2627-P04).

So in both, the share waiting more than 6 months dropped markedly in the latest year, and the share waiting more than a year has fallen by half or more over the three years. That's my reading of the figures above.

**Why I compare percentages for Québec, not counts:** Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec isn't reported in 2627-P04 but is in the earlier two periods (it reported 2,503 orthopedic patients in 2526-P04), so the provincial count partly falls just because a region is missing. Montréal is present in every period, so its counts are comparable.

**The latest period on its own is noise:** from period 3 to period 4, Montréal's orthopedic list rose by 213 (from 6,052 to 6,265), after falling by 296 the period before; the provincial list rose by 829 (the same 15 regions in both periods). One period is about 4 weeks.

**Limits.** These are end-of-period snapshots: period 4 ends in late July 2026, so the latest figures are over three months old. Wait time is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate; time spent getting to a specialist isn't included. These are regional totals, not per-hospital, and they don't predict any one person's wait. If you're waiting and worried about your condition, contact your surgeon's office, or Info-Santé 811.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" file, periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P04 and 2627-P03._
