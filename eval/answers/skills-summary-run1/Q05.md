**No. Not finding a clinic appointment tonight doesn't, by itself, mean you need the ER, and finding one wouldn't mean you don't.** Whether you need the ER depends on your symptoms, and that's a medical judgment this data can't make.

**What to do instead of deciding from the appointment listings**
- **If your symptoms could be serious** (trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, someone very drowsy or hard to wake, thoughts of suicide): call 911 or go to the nearest ER now.
- **If you're not sure whether it can wait until tomorrow:** call Info-Santé 811. A nurse answers 24/7 and can tell you whether to go in tonight or where to be seen.
- **For a minor problem:** a pharmacist can advise and, for some conditions, prescribe; your own family doctor or GMF can be called when it opens; without a family doctor, the GAP (Québec.ca or 811) can direct you. Rendez-vous santé Québec or a clinic's own booking system show actual slots.

**Late-night clinic appointments are rare around Ahuntsic anyway.** In MSSS's latest published week (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord):
- Only 1 clinic, District Médical, offered any physician appointments in the 21h–6h band: 25 over the week, spread over 7 of 7 days, so only a few per night. No nurse appointments were offered in that band at all.
- Earlier in the evening, between 18h and 21h, 9 clinics offered physician appointments on at least one day.
- That band runs through the night to 6h, so the data can't say how many of those were just after 9 pm. And these are past offers: they don't tell you whether anything is free tonight, and clinics outside the provincial booking hub aren't counted.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file, published weekly (Thursdays); data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, the latest available this morning. It is never current availability._
