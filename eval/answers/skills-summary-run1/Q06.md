**The open data can't tell you which ER would see you fastest, or how long you'd wait, and I won't guess.** ERs see people by triage priority, not arrival order, and MSSS warns that a low count of patients present or waiting doesn't mean being seen sooner.

**For a wait estimate:** Québec.ca's "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences) shows, for each ER, an estimated wait before seeing a physician for a *non-priority* case. It's an estimate that depends on the staff and patients present when you arrive, it doesn't apply to urgent cases, and it isn't a promise.

**For a sprain, there are also routes besides the ER.** Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, 24/7) can advise you on where to be seen; a pharmacist can advise on minor problems; your own clinic or GMF, or the GAP if you have no family doctor (Québec.ca or 811), can direct you; Rendez-vous santé Québec lists clinic appointments. Get seen in person promptly if you can't put any weight on the foot, the ankle looks deformed, the foot is numb, cold or pale, or the pain or swelling is severe.
