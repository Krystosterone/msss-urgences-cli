**For non-urgent ear pain around Ahuntsic you have several routes, and plenty of local clinics regularly offer appointments through the provincial booking system.**

**Ways to get a consultation**
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7, including whether and how soon you should be seen.
- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF,** if you have one.
- **No family doctor:** the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To book an actual slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

**Which clinics around Ahuntsic usually offer appointments** (RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord, MSSS data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, all 7 days with data):
- 15 clinics offered appointments through the provincial booking hub that week: 15 offered physician appointments (6,070 in all) and 9 offered nurse appointments (702 in all).
- 5 clinics offered physician appointments on every one of the 7 days: Centre médical Mieux-Être succ. Ahuntsic, CLSC Ahuntsic, Clinique médicale Omnisanté, District Médical and District Médical Junior.
- Evenings: 9 clinics offered physician appointments between 18h and 21h on at least one day. Among them, District Médical did so on 7 of 7 days, CLSC de Montréal-Nord on 5, and Clinique médicale Désy and District Médical Junior on 4 each.
- A clinic in a neighbouring RLS may be closer to you; this list only covers Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord.

**What this data can't tell you:** these are appointments clinics *offered* last week, not openings today. Some were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients, and clinics outside the booking hub aren't counted. A higher count means a clinic offers more appointments through the hub, not better odds for you.

**When it's no longer "nothing urgent":** call 811 or get seen promptly if you develop a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, sudden hearing loss or dizziness, or if the pain becomes severe. For confusion, trouble breathing or a person who is very drowsy, call 911.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file, published weekly (Thursdays); data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, the latest dates available this morning, so 8 to 14 days old._
