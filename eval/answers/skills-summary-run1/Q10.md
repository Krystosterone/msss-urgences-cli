**Yes, by its own norm: yesterday's stays at Maisonneuve-Rosemont's ER were noticeably longer than its average this fiscal year, even though its stretcher area is less full than most of Montréal's this morning.** The two measures answer different questions, and both are below.

- **Against its norm (yesterday vs this fiscal year).** Patients who left yesterday spent 29.4 h on a stretcher on average, 33% more than its 22.1 h mean since April 1 (fiscal year 2026-2027, through period 7). Walk-in (ambulatory) patients stayed 7.8 h, 35% more than its 5.8 h mean. Within about ±10% would be normal day-to-day noise; +25% or more is noticeably longer than usual, and both are past that. Note this is *yesterday's* stays (patients who left the ER yesterday, Wednesday), not today's, and not how long someone arriving now would stay.
- **Right now (live snapshot).** 133% stretcher occupancy (72 occupied on 54 functional stretchers), 30 people waiting to see a physician, 99 present in total, 31 on a stretcher for 24 h or more (8 of them for 48 h or more). Montréal's rate is 161% (MSSS's regional total) and the mean of Montréal's ERs is 152%, so Maisonneuve-Rosemont sits 19 points below that mean: busy, but less full than many Montréal ERs at this moment.
- **Against its recent hours: too thin to judge.** Its occupancy is 9.3 points below the only other snapshot at around 8h in the archive (142.6%), which starts on 2026-11-03; one snapshot isn't a norm.

**Its norm this year is itself heavier than last year's.** So far in 2026-2027 its mean stretcher stay is 22.1 h, against 18.6 h over the same April-to-period-7 window of 2025-2026 (+3.49 h); 36.6% of stretcher visits lasted 24 h or more (+8.1 points), and the average delay from triage to a first physician was 4.4 h (+0.83 h). That delay is an average over every patient since April 1, at all triage priorities, not anyone's expected wait.

None of these numbers is a wait time: patients are seen by triage priority. For Québec.ca's official estimate for non-priority cases, see the "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences).
