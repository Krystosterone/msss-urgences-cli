**What this can't tell you**
- These are head counts, not wait times. Children are seen by how urgent their condition is, not in arrival order, and MSSS warns that a low count doesn't mean being seen sooner. For an estimated wait for a non-priority case, see Québec.ca's "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences). It's an estimate that changes with the staff and patients present, it doesn't apply to urgent cases, and it isn't a promise.
- If your child is unwell and you're not sure whether to go, Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, 24/7) can advise you. If symptoms seem serious, call 911 or go to the nearest ER.
