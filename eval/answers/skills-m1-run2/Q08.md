**Orthopedic waitlists have shrunk over the past two years, both in Montréal and across Québec, and far fewer people are waiting more than a year. The latest period ticked up slightly in Montréal, but one period is too short to call a reversal.**

I compared four fiscal periods: period 4 (which ends in late July) of each of the last three fiscal years, plus period 3 of this one. Each figure is the number of people waiting since the surgeon's decision to operate, with their share of all orthopedic patients waiting.

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery**

| Period | Waiting over 6 months | Over 12 months | Total waiting |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 2,914 (35%) | 685 (8%) | 8,437 |
| 2526-P04 | 2,918 (37%) | 463 (6%) | 7,810 |
| 2627-P03 | 1,824 (30%) | 218 (4%) | 6,052 |
| 2627-P04 | 1,891 (30%) | 226 (4%) | 6,265 |

**Québec, orthopedic surgery**

| Period | Waiting over 6 months | Over 12 months | Total waiting |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 13,640 (35%) | 3,193 (8%) | 38,763 |
| 2526-P04 | 11,613 (34%) | 2,085 (6%) | 34,513 |
| 2627-P03* | 7,207 (25%) | 969 (3%) | 28,530 |
| 2627-P04* | 7,900 (27%) | 968 (3%) | 29,359 |

\* Excludes Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported in 2026-2027. It had 2,503 orthopedic patients waiting in 2526-P04, so part of the provincial drop in *counts* reflects its absence. The percentages are the fairer comparison.

**Reading it**
- **Montréal:** the share waiting over 6 months held at 35% to 37% in the two previous summers and is now 30%. The over-a-year group fell by about two-thirds over those two years.
- **Québec:** the over-6-months share went from 35% to 34%, then 27%, and the over-a-year share from 8% to 3%.
- **Montréal vs Québec:** in 2627-P04, 30% of Montréal's orthopedic patients had waited over 6 months, against 27% province-wide, so Montréal runs a bit above the provincial share.
- From 2627-P03 to 2627-P04, Montréal's list grew slightly. That's a single period (about four weeks), which is noise rather than a trend.

**Limits**
- These are end-of-period snapshots, and the latest, 2627-P04, ends in late July 2026, so it's more than three months old.
- The wait counts from the surgeon's decision to operate. Time spent getting a family doctor or a specialist appointment isn't included.
- These are regional totals, not per hospital, and they don't predict any one person's wait. If you're waiting for surgery and worried, your surgeon's office is the one to ask, or Info-Santé 811 for your health in the meantime.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" file, orthopedic surgery, periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04._
