**No. Not finding a clinic appointment after 9 pm doesn't mean you need the ER, and finding one wouldn't mean you don't. Whether you need the ER is a medical question, and appointment listings can't answer it.**

**Go to the ER or call 911 now, whatever the listings show,** if there is trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, someone very drowsy or hard to wake, a fever in a baby under 3 months, or thoughts of suicide.

**If you're not sure how serious it is, call Info-Santé 811.** A nurse answers 24/7 and can tell you whether to go to the ER tonight or whether it can wait for a regular appointment. Other routes:
- **A pharmacist** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **For an appointment:** Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.

**What MSSS's clinic data shows for your area** (RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord)
- It can't show tonight at all. MSSS publishes it weekly, and the latest date it covers is 2026-10-28.
- Late-night appointments are rare there anyway. Across the seven dates from 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, only one clinic, District Médical, offered any physician appointments scheduled between 21h and 6h: 3 on 2026-10-28, for example, and 6 on Saturday 2026-10-24. Most clinics' offers stop by 18h or 21h.
- So an empty evening is normal for the area. It says nothing about how urgent your situation is.
