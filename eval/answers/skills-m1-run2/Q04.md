**For non-urgent ear pain around Ahuntsic, you have several routes: Info-Santé 811, a pharmacist, your own family doctor or GMF if you have one, and the clinics in the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord local network that regularly offer appointments through the provincial booking hub.**

**Ways to get a consultation**
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7. They can also tell you whether and where you should be seen.
- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811, can direct you to a clinic. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To find an actual appointment:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

**Which nearby clinics usually offer appointments** (MSSS clinic file, RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord; I looked at seven dates, from 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28)
- On 2026-10-28, a Wednesday and the latest date MSSS has published, 15 clinics in the RLS offered appointments. Among them, CENTRE MÉDICAL MIEUX-ÊTRE SUCC. AHUNTSIC offered 172 physician appointments, District Médical 426, Clinique médicale Désy 113, Clinique médicale Urgence 241 Ahuntsic 58, and the CLSC AHUNTSIC 22 physician and 38 nurse appointments.
- **Evenings** (appointments scheduled between 18h and 21h): District Médical offered some on all seven dates (25 physician appointments on 2026-10-28). Clinique médicale Désy and District Médical Junior offered evening physician appointments on four of the seven dates.
- **Weekends are much thinner:** 7 clinics offered appointments on Saturday 2026-10-24 (215 appointments in all) and 7 on Sunday 2026-10-25 (179), against 15 clinics and 1372 appointments on Wednesday 2026-10-28.
- "Physician" appointments are MSSS's *Médecin* group (physicians and residents); "nurse" is *Infirmière* (nurses and specialized nurse practitioners). The order above isn't a ranking: more offers means a clinic puts more appointments on the hub, not better odds for you.

**What this data can't tell you**
- These are appointments that were *offered* on past dates, not openings now. Some were booked, some are reserved for the clinic's own patients, and clinics that aren't on the provincial hub aren't counted at all. A clinic in a neighbouring network may also be closer to you.

**When ear pain is no longer minor:** if it comes with a high fever, swelling, redness or pain behind the ear, a stiff neck, sudden hearing loss, severe dizziness, or you feel very unwell, call 811 or go to an ER; if it seems serious, call 911.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file (Portrait quotidien des rendez-vous en clinique), republished weekly on Thursdays; data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
