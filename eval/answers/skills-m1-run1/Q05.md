**No. Not finding a clinic appointment after 9 pm doesn't by itself mean you need the ER. Whether you do is a medical question that clinic listings can't answer.** It depends on your symptoms, not on which appointments are showing.

**First, if anything could be serious:** trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, or someone very drowsy or hard to wake. Then call 911 or go to the nearest ER now, whatever the clinics show.

**If it doesn't seem serious, or you're not sure:**
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse, available day and night, can tell you whether you should go to the ER tonight, see someone tomorrow, or manage at home.
- **A pharmacist** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one. Without a family doctor, the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), through Québec.ca or 811, can direct you to a clinic.
- To find an actual appointment, use Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.

**What the MSSS clinic data can and can't say here:** it never shows tonight. MSSS publishes it weekly, and the latest date it covers is 2026-10-28. It counts appointments that clinics on the provincial booking hub *offered* in the past, not what's free now, and clinics outside the hub aren't counted. For what it's worth, late-night clinic slots are rare around Ahuntsic. On the 7 dates I looked at (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28), only District Médical, in the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord, offered any physician appointments scheduled between 21h and 6h. It offered 3 of them on 2026-10-28. Those were past offers, not a guarantee of anything tonight.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file (published weekly); data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
