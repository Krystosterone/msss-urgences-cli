The fullest hours were in the early morning: around 3h, 4h, 7h and 8h each averaged 128.8%. Over the whole window, Verdun's occupancy ranged from 73.1% to 134.6%, with a mean of 115.7%.

**Why this isn't a pattern yet:**
- The archive began on 2026-11-03T22:45 and holds only 35 hourly snapshots. Each hour has just 1 or 2 snapshots, where about 3 or more are needed to call anything usual. A day and a half of snapshots can easily be unusual.
- None of this is weekday-specific. The data has no repeat of any weekday yet, so it can't say whether Mondays differ from Saturdays.
- It describes how full the stretcher area was, not how fast anyone was seen. A less crowded hour isn't a promise of a shorter wait. Patients are seen by triage priority, and MSSS warns that a low count doesn't mean being seen sooner.
- It's never a reason to delay care. If symptoms could be serious, go now (or call 911). If you're unsure, call Info-Santé 811.
