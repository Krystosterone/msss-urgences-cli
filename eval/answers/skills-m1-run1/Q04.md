**For non-urgent ear pain you have several options in and around Ahuntsic, and MSSS data shows that many local clinics regularly offer appointments, some of them in the evening.**

**Ways to get care for a non-urgent problem**
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse can advise you by phone, day or night, including on whether and how soon you need to be seen.
- **A pharmacist:** they can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811, can direct you to a clinic. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To book a slot:** use Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.

**Which clinics around Ahuntsic usually offer appointments**
I looked at the MSSS clinic file for the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord on 7 dates, from 2026-10-22 (a Thursday) to 2026-10-28, the latest date MSSS has published:
- On 2026-10-28, 15 clinics in this RLS offered appointments through the provincial booking hub.
- Five clinics offered physician appointments on every date I looked at: District Médical, District Médical Junior, Centre médical Mieux-Être succ. Ahuntsic, CLSC Ahuntsic and Clinique médicale Omnisanté.
- Several regularly offered **evening physician appointments** (scheduled between 18h and 21h). On 2026-10-28, District Médical offered 426 physician appointments, 25 of them in the evening. Clinique médicale Désy offered 113, with 11 in the evening. District Médical Junior offered 143, with 8 in the evening. The CLSC de Montréal-Nord offered 53, with 5 in the evening.
- Some clinics also offer **nurse appointments** (nurses and specialized nurse practitioners). On 2026-10-28, these included 46 at Clinique médicale Désy and 38 at the CLSC Ahuntsic.
- Weekends are thinner. Only 7 clinics offered appointments on Saturday 2026-10-24.

**Limits of this data:** these are appointments that were *offered* a week or more ago. They don't show what's free today. Some were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients, and clinics that aren't on the provincial booking hub aren't counted. A clinic in a neighbouring RLS may also be closer to you.

**When it's no longer a minor problem:** a high fever, severe or rapidly worsening pain, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, confusion or unusual drowsiness, or sudden hearing loss or dizziness. If any of these appear, call 811 for advice, or go to the ER or call 911 if it seems serious.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file (published weekly, Thursdays); data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
