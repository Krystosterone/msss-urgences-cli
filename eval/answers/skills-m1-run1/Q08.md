**Orthopedic surgery waitlists have shrunk over the past two years, and long waits have fallen the most, both in Montréal and across Québec.** Comparing the same point in three fiscal years (period 4, which ends in late July), the share of orthopedic patients waiting more than 6 months fell from 35% to 30% in Montréal and from 35% to 27% province-wide. Montréal's share is still a bit above the province's.

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery**

| Period (fiscal year) | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 (2024-2025) | 8,437 | 2,914 (35%) | 685 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 (2025-2026) | 7,810 | 2,918 (37%) | 463 (6%) |
| 2627-P04 (2026-2027) | 6,265 | 1,891 (30%) | 226 (4%) |

**Québec (sum of the regions MSSS reports), orthopedic surgery**

| Period (fiscal year) | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 (2024-2025) | 38,763 | 13,640 (35%) | 3,193 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 (2025-2026) | 34,513 | 11,613 (34%) | 2,085 (6%) |
| 2627-P04 (2026-2027) * | 29,359 | 7,900 (27%) | 968 (3%) |

\* Excludes Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec (RSS04), which isn't reported in fiscal 2026-2027.

**Reading it**
- **Montréal:** the share waiting over 6 months rose slightly in 2025-2026 and then fell to 30% in 2627-P04. The number waiting over a year fell by about two-thirds over the two years (685 → 226).
- **Québec:** the over-6-month share fell from 35% to 27%, and the over-a-year share from 8% to 3%.
  - The provincial *counts* aren't fully comparable, because the latest year excludes Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec. That region had 2,503 people on the orthopedic list in 2526-P04, 34% of them for over 6 months. Part of the provincial drop is that missing region, so compare the percentages.
- **Montréal vs Québec:** in 2627-P04, 30% of Montréal's orthopedic patients had waited over 6 months, against 27% province-wide.
- **The most recent period-to-period change is just one period:** Montréal's orthopedic list went from 6,052 in 2627-P03 to 6,265 in 2627-P04. One period is noise, not a trend.

**Limits:** these are snapshots taken at the end of each period. The latest (2627-P04, the most recent MSSS has published) is about three months old. The wait is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate, so time spent getting a family doctor or a specialist appointment isn't included. These are regional totals that don't predict any one person's wait. If you're waiting and worried about your condition, your surgeon's office, or Info-Santé 811, is the place to ask.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies – portrait de la liste d'attente" file, orthopedic surgery, periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04._
