**For non-urgent ear pain around Ahuntsic you have several routes, and many clinics in the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord local network regularly offer appointments through the provincial booking hub, some in the evening.**

Ways to get care:

- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe. Often the quickest first stop.
- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7, including on where to go.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor:** the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811 (check Québec.ca for current eligibility).
- **To book an actual slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.

What the MSSS clinic data shows for the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord (I looked at the 7 latest dates published, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28):

- On 2026-10-28, the latest date, clinics on the hub offered 1372 appointments across 21 clinic listings (a clinic offering both physician and nurse appointments counts twice).
- **District Médical** offers the most physician appointments: 426 on 2026-10-28, 25 of them in the 18h–21h band. It offered evening (18h–21h) physician appointments on all 7 dates I looked at.
- **Clinique médicale Désy:** 113 physician appointments on 2026-10-28, 11 of them in the 18h–21h band, plus 46 nurse appointments.
- **CLSC Ahuntsic:** 22 physician and 38 nurse appointments on 2026-10-28.
- Other clinics with offers that day include Centre médical Mieux-Être (Ahuntsic), Clinique médicale Omnisanté, Clinique médicale Rayons De Soleil, District Médical Junior and Clinique médicale Urgence 241 Ahuntsic.
- Weekends are much thinner: 7 clinic listings on Saturday 2026-10-24 and 7 on Sunday 2026-10-25.

What this data can't tell you: these are appointments *offered* on past dates, not openings now. Some were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients, and clinics not on the hub aren't counted. MSSS republishes the file weekly (Thursdays at 14h), so the newest date is 2026-10-28; use the booking routes above to find what's actually free. Depending on where you are in Ahuntsic, a clinic in a neighbouring network may be closer.

Taking you at your word that it's not urgent: get care promptly (811, or the ER if it's severe) if you develop a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, sudden hearing loss, dizziness or weakness of the face.
