**No. Not finding a clinic appointment after 9 pm doesn't mean you need the ER, and the clinic data can't decide that either way. Whether your ear pain can wait until tomorrow is a medical question: Info-Santé 811 can help you answer it tonight.**

- **The clinic data never shows tonight.** MSSS's clinic file counts appointments that clinics on the provincial booking hub *offered* on past dates. It's republished weekly (Thursdays at 14h), and the newest date in it is 2026-10-28, so it can't list, or rule out, anything for tonight. It also leaves out clinics that aren't on the hub.
- **Late appointments do exist in the pattern.** In the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord, District Médical offered physician appointments in the 21h–6h band on each of the 7 latest dates published, from 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28 (3 of them on 2026-10-28). That shows a clinic that sometimes schedules late, not an opening tonight: check its own booking system or Rendez-vous santé Québec.
- **Other routes tonight:** call **Info-Santé 811** (a nurse, 24/7) for advice on whether and where to be seen; a **pharmacist** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe (check your local pharmacy's hours). Tomorrow, your family doctor or GMF if you have one, or the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP) through Québec.ca or 811 if you don't.
- **When it would change:** go to the ER, or call 911 if it's severe, if you develop a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, a severe headache, confusion, sudden hearing loss, dizziness or weakness of the face. If you're unsure, 811 first.

And the reverse holds too: had there been appointments listed, that wouldn't have meant you could skip the ER if your symptoms called for it.
