**Long waits for orthopedic surgery are clearly shorter than in past years, in Montréal and across Québec, with most of the improvement in the last year. Montréal now runs a little above the provincial share of patients waiting over 6 months (30% vs 27% in the latest period, 2627-P04).**

I compared four fiscal-period snapshots: period 4 of each of the last three fiscal years (2024-2025, 2025-2026, 2026-2027), plus the period just before the latest one (2627-P03).

Montréal, orthopedic surgery (people waiting · over 6 months · over a year):

| Period | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 8,437 | 2,914 (35%) | 685 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 7,810 | 2,918 (37%) | 463 (6%) |
| 2627-P03 | 6,052 | 1,824 (30%) | 218 (4%) |
| 2627-P04 | 6,265 | 1,891 (30%) | 226 (4%) |

All of Québec, orthopedic surgery (sum of the regions MSSS reports):

| Period | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 38,763 | 13,640 (35%) | 3,193 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 34,513 | 11,613 (34%) | 2,085 (6%) |
| 2627-P03 | 28,530 | 7,207 (25%) | 969 (3%) |
| 2627-P04 | 29,359 | 7,900 (27%) | 968 (3%) |

What I read in it:

- **Year over year, long waits shrank, mostly in the last year.** In Montréal the share waiting over 6 months was 35% (2425-P04), then 37% a year later (2526-P04), before falling to 30% (2627-P04), and the number waiting over a year from 685 to 226. Province-wide the over-6-month share went from 35% to 27%, and the over-a-year share from 8% to 3%.
- **The last period ticked up slightly** (Montréal from 6,052 to 6,265 people waiting; Québec's over-6-month share from 25% to 27%), but one period (~4 weeks) is noise, not a trend.
- **Careful with the provincial counts across years:** in fiscal 2026-2027 (2627-P03 and 2627-P04), Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec isn't reported, so the provincial figures for those periods leave it out, while the earlier ones include it. Part of the drop in the provincial count comes from that missing region; the percentages and Montréal's own figures are the fairer comparison.

Limits: these are end-of-period snapshots (MSSS publishes 13 periods a year), not today's list. Wait time is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate, so time spent getting to a family doctor or a specialist isn't included. They are regional totals, not per hospital, and don't predict any one person's wait: the surgeon's office is the authority on that. (Cardiac surgery is only partly covered by the source system; that doesn't affect orthopedics.) If you're waiting and your condition worsens, contact your surgeon's office or Info-Santé 811.
