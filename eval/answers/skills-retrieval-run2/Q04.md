**For a non-urgent ear problem you have several routes, and many clinics in the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord network regularly offer appointments, some in the evening.**

**Ways to get care**

- **A pharmacist** can advise you on ear pain and, for some conditions, can prescribe.
- **Info-Santé 811** gives a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor:** the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To find an actual appointment:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

**Which nearby clinics have been offering appointments** (MSSS clinic file, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28; all 7 days in that window have data):

- 15 clinics in the RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord offered appointments through the provincial booking hub that week.
- District Médical offered physician appointments every day of the window. On 2026-10-28 it offered 426, including 25 between 18h and 21h.
- Clinique médicale Désy offered 113 physician appointments on 2026-10-28, 11 of them between 18h and 21h, and also offered nurse appointments (46 that day).
- District Médical Junior offered 143 physician appointments on 2026-10-28, 8 of them in the evening.
- CLSC Ahuntsic offered 22 physician appointments and 38 nurse appointments on 2026-10-28.
- Clinique médicale Urgence 241 Ahuntsic offered 58 physician appointments on 2026-10-28, all before 18h.

"Physician" covers physicians and residents (MSSS's `Médecin` group). "Nurse" covers nurses and specialized nurse practitioners (`Infirmière`). A clinic in a neighbouring network might be closer to you than some of these.

**What this data can't tell you.** These are appointments offered about a week ago, not openings now. MSSS republishes the file weekly (Thursdays at 14h) up to the day before. The counts don't say whether a slot was booked or who may book it (some are only for the clinic's own patients), and clinics outside the booking hub aren't counted. More offers means a clinic that puts more appointments on the hub, not better odds for you.

**When it's no longer minor:** if you develop a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck or severe headache, sudden hearing loss or dizziness, or you feel very unwell, call 811 for advice or go to an ER. For anything that seems serious, call 911.

_Source: MSSS "Portrait quotidien des rendez-vous en clinique" file, published weekly; data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
