**Orthopedic waitlists have shrunk, and long waits have become less common, in Montréal and across Québec, comparing the same period over the last three fiscal years.** In Montréal, the share of orthopedic patients waiting over 6 months fell from 37% to 30% in the past year. Province-wide it fell from 34% to 27%.

If you're worried about your own health while waiting for surgery, contact your surgeon's office, or call Info-Santé 811.

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery** (period 4 of each fiscal year)

| Period | People waiting | Over 6 months | Share over 6 months | Over 12 months | Share over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 8,437 | 2,914 | 35% | 685 | 8% |
| 2526-P04 | 7,810 | 2,918 | 37% | 463 | 6% |
| 2627-P04 | 6,265 | 1,891 | 30% | 226 | 4% |

**All of Québec, orthopedic surgery** (sum of the regions MSSS reports)

| Period | People waiting | Over 6 months | Share over 6 months | Over 12 months | Share over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 38,763 | 13,640 | 35% | 3,193 | 8% |
| 2526-P04 | 34,513 | 11,613 | 34% | 2,085 | 6% |
| 2627-P04 | 29,359 | 7,900 | 27% | 968 | 3% |

- **Lead with the long waits.** Both Montréal and the province have far fewer people waiting over a year than two years ago, and a smaller share waiting over 6 months. Montréal's share over 6 months (30%) is now a bit above the provincial share (27%).
- **Watch the provincial count.** The 2627-P04 provincial figures exclude Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported in that period but was included in the two earlier years. Part of the drop in the provincial *count* comes from that missing region, so the shares are the fairer comparison.
- **Most recent period:** from 2627-P03 to 2627-P04, Montréal's orthopedic list went from 6,052 to 6,265 people, with the share over 6 months steady at 30%. Province-wide (the same regions in both periods) it went from 28,530 to 29,359. A single period (about a month) is noise, not a trend.

**Limits.** These counts are end-of-period snapshots. Period 4 of fiscal 2026-2027 ended in late July, so the latest data is more than three months old, and no later period has been published yet. The wait is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate, not from the first visit to a doctor or specialist. These are regional totals, not per-hospital figures, and they don't predict anyone's own wait. The surgeon's office is the authority on that.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" file, periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04 and 2627-P04 (plus 2627-P03 for the latest change)._
