**I can't tell you which ER will see you fastest or how long you'd wait. The open data has no wait times, and crowding doesn't predict who is seen sooner.** Here's what can help.

**How long you'd wait.** Québec.ca's "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences) shows MSSS's estimated wait per ER before a non-priority case sees a physician. It's an estimate that depends on the staff and patients there when you arrive, it doesn't apply to urgent cases, and it isn't a promise. The CLI data I have doesn't carry it, and I won't make up a number.

**Other options for a sprain.** You may not need an ER at all:

- A **pharmacist** can advise on a sprain and on pain relief.
- **Info-Santé 811** (a nurse, 24/7) can tell you whether it needs an X-ray or an ER.
- Your **family doctor or GMF**, or, without one, the **GAP** (Québec.ca or 811). To book a clinic appointment, use **Rendez-vous santé Québec** or the clinic's own booking system.

**Go to an ER, or call 911, if** you can't put any weight on it, the ankle looks deformed, the foot is numb, cold or pale, or the pain or swelling is severe.
