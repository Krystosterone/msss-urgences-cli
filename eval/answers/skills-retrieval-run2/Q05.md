**No. Not finding a clinic appointment doesn't mean you need the ER, and the clinic data can't make that call for you. Whether you need the ER depends on your symptoms.**

- **If anything could be serious** (trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, someone very drowsy or hard to wake, thoughts of suicide), call 911 or go to the nearest ER now.
- **If you're not sure how serious it is,** call Info-Santé 811. A nurse answers 24/7 and can tell you whether you need the ER tonight, a clinic tomorrow, or care at home.
- **Other routes for a minor problem:** a pharmacist (who can advise and, for some conditions, prescribe), your own family doctor or GMF, or, without a family doctor, the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP) at Québec.ca or through 811. To look for an actual appointment, use Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.

**Why the clinic data can't show "tonight"**

MSSS publishes its clinic file weekly (Thursdays at 14h), up to the day before. The newest data I have is for 2026-10-28, so it says nothing about tonight. It counts appointments that clinics on the provincial booking hub *offered* for past dates: not whether they were booked, not who could book them, and nothing about clinics outside the hub.

What it does show is that late appointments are rare around Ahuntsic. Over 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, in the RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord:

- Only District Médical offered physician appointments scheduled after 9 pm (MSSS's 21h–6h band), and it did so every day of that window. It offered 3 on 2026-10-28, and 2 on Thursday 2026-10-22.
- A few more clinics offered evening (18h–21h) physician appointments. On Thursday 2026-10-22 these were District Médical (5), Clinique médicale Désy (8), Clinique Familiale Pas-à-Pas (6) and District Médical Junior (2).

So seeing nothing after 9 pm is normal for this area and says nothing about how urgent your problem is. These are past offers, not openings now. If you think you need care tonight and can't tell how urgent it is, call 811.

_Source: MSSS "Portrait quotidien des rendez-vous en clinique" file, published weekly; data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
