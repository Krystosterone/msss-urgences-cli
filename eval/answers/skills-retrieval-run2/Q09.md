What this can and can't tell you:

- It describes past crowding on a very short record, not a pattern you can plan around yet, and it says nothing about which weekday is calmer. In a few weeks, `quebec-sante urgences-historique --id 51218097 --by weekday-hour --days 28` would give a weekday-by-hour profile with enough samples to be meaningful.
- Lower occupancy means a less full stretcher area, not a faster visit. ERs see people by triage priority, and the numbers don't show staffing.
- It's never a reason to put off care. If something could be serious, call 911 or go to the nearest ER now. If you're unsure, Info-Santé 811 can advise you.
