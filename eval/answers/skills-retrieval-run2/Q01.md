Three views answer three different questions, so here is each one separately:

- **Right now (live MSSS file).** 88% stretcher occupancy (14 of 16 functional stretchers occupied), 9 children waiting to see a physician, 33 patients present, and none on a stretcher for over 24 h. Montréal's regional rate is 161% (MSSS's regional total), and the mean of Montréal's ERs is 152%, so Sainte-Justine sits well below that mean (Δ −64). That mean mixes adult and pediatric ERs. Montréal's other pediatric ER, the Montreal Children's, is at 183%.
- **Against its own norm this fiscal year (`--baseline`).** Patients who left yesterday spent 6.6 h on a stretcher on average, 2% more than its 6.4 h average so far in fiscal 2026-2027 (April 1 to period 7). That's within normal day-to-day noise. Walk-in (ambulatory) stays were 4.3 h vs 4.1 h, +3%, also normal. This describes yesterday's patients, not a child arriving now.
- **Against the same hour in recent history (`urgences-historique`).** The archive only starts at 2026-11-03 22:45, so the 28-day window holds just 35 hourly snapshots. The 08h slot has 2 snapshots, so the "typical" figure for this hour (68.8%) rests on one other snapshot, and there's no same-weekday (Thursday) figure at all. The +18.7-point gap from that typical figure means nothing yet: the history is too thin to call a pattern.

**My reading:** the live numbers and the baseline both say this isn't an unusually crowded morning for Sainte-Justine. There isn't enough history to compare it with other Thursday mornings yet. None of this says how quickly a child would be seen. Children are seen by how urgent their condition is, and MSSS warns that a low count doesn't mean being seen sooner. For an estimated wait, Québec.ca's "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences) shows MSSS's estimate per ER for a non-priority case. It depends on the staff and patients present when you arrive, doesn't apply to urgent cases, and isn't a promise. If a child's symptoms could be serious, call 911 or go to the nearest ER. If you're unsure, Info-Santé 811 can advise you.
