**Several clinics in the Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord area regularly offer non-urgent appointments through the provincial booking hub, on weekdays especially, and a few of them in the evening.** To actually get a consultation for your ear:

- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse can advise you by phone, around the clock, including on whether and how soon you should be seen.
- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one: call or use their booking system first.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811, can direct you to a clinic. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To find a slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

- Evening appointments (scheduled between 18h and 21h) were offered by District Médical (25 evening), Clinique médicale Désy (11 evening), District Médical Junior (8 evening), Clinique médicale Rayons De Soleil (7 evening), CLSC de Montréal-Nord (5 evening) and CLSC de Montréal-Nord-Est (4 evening) with physicians, and by Clinique médicale Omnisanté with a nurse (3 evening).
- Weekends are much thinner: only 7 clinics offered anything on each of 2026-10-24 and 2026-10-25.

**What this can't tell you:** these are appointments offered a week or more ago, not openings now — MSSS publishes this file weekly, and its newest date is 2026-10-28. Some of those slots were booked, some are reserved for a clinic's own patients, and clinics that aren't on the provincial booking hub don't appear at all. A clinic offering more appointments isn't one where you're more likely to get one.

**When it would stop being a "non-urgent" problem:** call 811 or get seen promptly if the pain becomes severe, or comes with a high fever, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, sudden hearing loss, dizziness or weakness of the face; call 911 or go to the nearest ER if you feel very unwell or confused.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file (appointments offered through the provincial booking hub), published weekly; dates 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
