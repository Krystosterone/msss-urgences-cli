**Not steadily. On the strain indicators MSSS publishes for fiscal years, Montréal's ERs got worse into 2022-2023, then eased, and by 2025-2026 were back close to their 2021-2022 level. This year's figures can't be compared yet, because fewer Montréal ERs are in them.**

What I used: MSSS's cumulative ER file has no stretcher-occupancy rate, so "overcrowding" here means its strain indicators — how long patients stay on a stretcher, the share of stretcher stays lasting 24 h or 48 h or more, and the average delay from triage to a first physician. Each year covers the same window, April 1 to the end of period 7 (roughly mid-October), for the Montréal region as a whole.

| Fiscal year (Apr. → period 7) | ERs in the data | Mean stretcher stay | Stretcher stays 24 h+ | Stretcher stays 48 h+ | Mean delay to a physician |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2021-2022 | 21 ERs | 18.0 h | 25.2% | 6.7% | 2.6 h |
| 2022-2023 | 21 ERs | 19.9 h | 27.3% | 9.2% | 2.8 h |
| 2023-2024 | 21 ERs | 19.2 h | 26.7% | 7.8% | 2.7 h |
| 2024-2025 | 21 ERs | 18.7 h | 25.9% | 7.2% | 2.8 h |
| 2025-2026 | 21 ERs | 18.4 h | 24.6% | 6.7% | 2.6 h |
| 2026-2027 (not comparable) | 17 ERs | 19.0 h | 23.9% | 7.3% | 2.7 h |

Lower is better in every indicator column.

- **2021-2022 → 2022-2023: worse.** Longer stretcher stays, a larger share of 24 h+ and 48 h+ stays, a longer delay to a physician.
- **2022-2023 → 2025-2026: better each year on stretcher stays and on the 24 h+ share,** ending slightly below the 2021-2022 share of 24 h+ stays, with the 48 h+ share back where it was. The delay to a physician moved up and down but ended at about the same level.
- So across these five comparable years (all with the same number of ERs), I'd describe it as a bad patch in 2022-2023 and a return toward the earlier level, not a steady worsening.
- **2026-2027 so far:** the region's figures cover 17 ERs instead of 21 in every earlier year, so they aren't comparable — the change in the totals could just reflect which ERs are in them. The live ER report lists 4 Montréal ERs with no row for this fiscal year: Hôpital du Sacré-Cœur, Hôpital Fleury, Hôpital Jean-Talon and the Albert-Prévost psychiatric ER (probably, though not certainly, the same four missing from the regional total). That is missing data, not closures.

**What this can't show:** these are averages over months of past visits, not anyone's wait. Live stretcher-occupancy history does exist, but the archive only starts at 2026-11-03T22:45 (35 snapshots so far), so it says nothing about change over time yet. Right now (published 2026-11-05T08:45), Montréal's regional occupancy rate is 161%.

_Sources: MSSS cumulative ER file ("Fichier cumulatif des données des urgences"), fiscal years 2021-2022 to 2026-2027, each cumulated April 1 → period 7; MSSS hourly ER file published 2026-11-05T08:45 for the live figures._
