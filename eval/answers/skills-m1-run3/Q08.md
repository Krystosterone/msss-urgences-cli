**Orthopedic waitlists are shorter now than at the same point in the two previous years, both in Montréal and across Québec, and the share of patients waiting more than 6 months has fallen.** In the latest period, Montréal's orthopedic waitlist is 6,265 people, 30% of them waiting over 6 months.

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery** (people waiting, by time since the surgeon decided to operate)

| Period | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over a year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 8,437 | 2,914 (35%) | 685 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 7,810 | 2,918 (37%) | 463 (6%) |
| 2627-P03 | 6,052 | 1,824 (30%) | 218 (4%) |
| 2627-P04 | 6,265 | 1,891 (30%) | 226 (4%) |

**Québec as a whole, orthopedic surgery** (sum of the regions reported)

| Period | Waiting | Over 6 months | Over a year |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 38,763 | 13,640 (35%) | 3,193 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 34,513 | 11,613 (34%) | 2,085 (6%) |
| 2627-P03 | 28,530 | 7,207 (25%) | 969 (3%) |
| 2627-P04 | 29,359 | 7,900 (27%) | 968 (3%) |

- **Same period, year over year (period 4 of each fiscal year):** in Montréal the long-wait share went from 35% to 37% and then down to 30%, and the share waiting over a year fell steadily, from 8% to 6% to 4%. Province-wide the share over 6 months went from 35% to 34% to 27%.
- **Latest period vs the one before:** Montréal edged up from 6,052 to 6,265; one fiscal period is noise, so I wouldn't read a new trend into it.
- **Montréal vs Québec:** in the latest period Montréal's share over 6 months (30%) is a little above the provincial share (27%), and its share over a year (4%) is a little above Québec's (3%).

**Caveats**
- **A region is missing.** Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec is not reported in either 2026-2027 period, so the provincial figures for 2627-P03 and 2627-P04 exclude it; it had 2,503 orthopedic patients waiting in 2526-P04. Part of the drop in the provincial count is therefore just its absence — the percentages are the fairer comparison.
- **These are old snapshots.** The latest data is period 4 of fiscal 2026-2027, which ended in late July; counts are taken at the end of each period.
- The clock starts at the surgeon's decision to operate: time spent getting a referral or a specialist appointment isn't counted. These are regional totals, not per-hospital, and they don't predict any one person's wait — the surgeon's office is the authority on that, and anyone worried about their condition while waiting should contact it, or Info-Santé 811.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies — Portrait de la liste d'attente" file, periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04 (end-of-period snapshots)._
