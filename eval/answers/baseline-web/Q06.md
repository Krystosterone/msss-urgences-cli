First, a sprained ankle usually doesn't need an ER. If you can put weight on it and there's no bone tenderness at the ankle knobs or the midfoot, rest, ice, compression and elevation, plus a clinic or physio visit, is usually enough. If you can't take four steps, or the bone is tender, you'll want an X-ray. A walk-in clinic or GMF with X-ray access is usually faster than an ER for that.
