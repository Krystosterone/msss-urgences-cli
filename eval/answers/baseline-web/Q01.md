Doctr's typical profile covers all days, not Thursday mornings only, and it doesn't say what period it averages over. I couldn't find an official Thursday-morning baseline, so read this as a rough comparison.

Caveats:
- These are hourly snapshots and can change quickly.
- Triage sees urgent cases first, whatever the occupancy.
- Occupancy measures stretcher use, not your personal wait.
