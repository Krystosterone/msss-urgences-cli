For non-urgent ear pain around Ahuntsic, here are the main options. Hours come from directory listings, so call before you go.

**Walk-in and family-medicine clinics (GMF) in or near Ahuntsic**
- **Clinique médicale Urgence 241 Ahuntsic (GMF):** 10302, rue Chambord, local 106 (near Fleury / Saint-Hubert), 514-382-0062.
  - Listed as walk-in.
  - Hours per Doctr: Mon–Wed and Fri–Sat 9 am–3 pm, **Thursday 9 am–7 pm**, closed Sunday.
- **Clinique médicale Saint-Sulpice (GMF Ahuntsic):** 1420, rue Legendre Est, local 12, 514-382-0552.
  - Online booking or walk-in.
  - Hours per Doctr: Mon–Thu 8:30 am–6 pm, Fri 8:30 am–4 pm.
- **GMF Centre médical Mieux-Être Ahuntsic:** 1611, boul. Crémazie Est, 7th floor.
- **CLSC d'Ahuntsic:** 1165, boul. Henri-Bourassa Est. Medical visits there are by appointment.

**Booking same-day or next-day appointments**
- **Rendez-vous santé Québec (rvsq.gouv.qc.ca):** the government booking site, with your RAMQ card. It shows open slots at nearby clinics, including for people without a family doctor.
- **Bonjour-santé** and **Clic Santé** are private booking platforms many clinics use.

**If you don't have a family doctor**
- **Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP):** fill in the form at Québec.ca/GuichetAcces, or call **811, option 3**. Someone calls you back and directs you to a clinic, nurse or other professional.

**Advice by phone, 24/7**
- **Info-Santé 811, option 1:** a nurse can tell you whether you need to be seen and how soon.

**Pharmacist**
- A good first stop for pain relief (acetaminophen or ibuprofen) and advice, for example about swimmer's ear or wax.
- Québec pharmacists can prescribe for some minor conditions. I couldn't confirm which ear problems are covered, so ask.

Go to the ER instead if you have any of these:
- high fever, or severe or worsening pain
- swelling or redness behind the ear
- facial weakness
- a stiff neck or confusion
- sudden hearing loss or severe dizziness
- bloody discharge after an injury
