**Orthopedic surgery waitlists have shrunk a lot from their pandemic-era peak, both in Montréal and across Québec.** The drop in patients waiting more than a year is especially large.

Figures are from the MSSS open-data file "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente". The latest period available is **2026-27 P04 (June 28 – July 25, 2026)**.

**Montréal (RSS 06), orthopedics**

| Period | Total waiting | > 1 year | Share > 1 year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar 2020 (pre-pandemic, 2019-20 P13) | 7,183 | 135 | 1.9% |
| Jul 2022 (2022-23 P04) | 9,239 | **1,764** | 19.1% |
| Mar 2023 (2022-23 P13, peak size) | **10,115** | 1,674 | 16.5% |
| Jul 2025 (2025-26 P04) | 7,810 | 463 | 5.9% |
| **Jul 2026 (2026-27 P04)** | **6,265** | **226** | 3.6% |

**All of Québec (sum of regions), orthopedics**

| Period | Total waiting | > 1 year | Share > 1 year |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mar 2020 | 28,933 | 771 | 2.7% |
| Mar 2022 | 38,371 | **6,324** | 16.5% |
| Mar 2024 (peak size) | **39,576** | 3,719 | 9.4% |
| Jul 2025 | 34,513 | 2,085 | 6.0% |
| **Jul 2026** | **29,359** | **968** | 3.3% |

What this means:
- **Montréal:** the list is now smaller than before the pandemic, down about 38% from its peak and about 20% in the past year. Patients waiting more than a year fell from about 1,760 to about 230.
- **Québec:** the list is back to roughly pre-pandemic size (about 29,400), down about 26% from the 2024 peak. More-than-a-year waits are down about 85% from the 2022 peak, though still above the 2020 level of 771.
- Orthopedics is still the largest surgical waitlist in the province. Total waiting across all specialties was about 126,000 in July 2026.

Caveats:
- My Québec totals add up the regional rows. Small suppressed cells are counted as zero.
- The data is about 3–4 months old.
- Some surgeons have warned the orthopedic list may be **under-counted** because some sites stopped accepting new requests. A shorter official list doesn't necessarily mean shorter waits to get *onto* it.
