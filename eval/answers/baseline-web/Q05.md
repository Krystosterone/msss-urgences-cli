**No.** No appointments showing after 9 pm doesn't mean you need the ER. Most clinics around Ahuntsic close by 6–7 pm. Clinic hours online and the booking sites mostly show daytime and early-evening slots, so an empty evening is normal and says nothing about how urgent your problem is.

What decides whether you need the ER is your **symptoms**, not appointment availability. For non-urgent ear pain:

1. **Call Info-Santé 811 (option 1, 24/7) tonight.** A nurse will assess you by phone. They'll tell you whether it can wait until tomorrow, whether you should see someone tonight, or whether you need the ER.
2. **Manage the pain tonight.** Acetaminophen or ibuprofen if they're safe for you, and avoid putting anything in the ear. A pharmacist (some pharmacies are open late) can advise you.
3. **Book for tomorrow morning:**
   - Rendez-vous santé Québec, Bonjour-santé or Clic Santé, which often release new slots early in the day.
   - Or a walk-in: Clinique médicale Urgence 241 Ahuntsic (10302 rue Chambord) opens at 9 am and Clinique Saint-Sulpice (1420 Legendre Est) at 8:30 am, per their Doctr listings.
4. **If you have no family doctor**, use the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (Québec.ca/GuichetAcces or 811, option 3) to be directed to a clinic.

**Go to the ER, or call 911 if severe, if you develop any of these:**
- high fever with severe pain
- swelling or redness behind the ear, or the ear pushed outward
- facial drooping
- stiff neck, confusion or severe headache
- sudden hearing loss or severe vertigo
- bleeding or discharge after an injury

Montréal ERs are very congested this morning (161% stretcher occupancy on average at 08:45). A non-urgent ear problem would likely face a long wait there, which is another reason to use 811 and a clinic.
