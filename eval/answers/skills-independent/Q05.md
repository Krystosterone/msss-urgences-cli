**No. Not finding a clinic appointment tonight doesn't mean you need the ER, and the clinic data can't decide that for you. That's a medical question, and Info-Santé 811 can help you answer it.**

- If your symptoms could be serious (trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, being very drowsy or hard to wake), call 911 or go to the nearest ER now, whatever the clinic listings show.
- If you're unsure how serious it is, call **Info-Santé 811**: a nurse answers day and night and can tell you whether to go to the ER, see a clinic tomorrow, or manage at home for now.

**Why an empty late evening doesn't tell you much**

- MSSS's clinic file can't show tonight at all. It is published weekly, its latest date is 2026-10-28, and it counts appointments clinics *offered* through the provincial booking hub, not what's free now.
- Appointments after 21h are rare around Ahuntsic anyway. Across the seven latest dates (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28), District Médical was the only clinic in the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord that offered any physician appointments scheduled after 21h: 3 on 2026-10-28, and between 1 (2026-10-26) and 6 (2026-10-24) on the other dates. So finding nothing tonight is the normal picture, not a sign about your health.

**Other options**

- **A pharmacist** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one; without one, the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), at Québec.ca or through 811.
- **For an appointment tomorrow:** Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system. In that file, District Médical offered physician appointments on each of those seven dates, including 25 scheduled in the evening on 2026-10-28.
