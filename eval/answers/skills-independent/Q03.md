**A very drowsy child with a fever needs care now: call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away. Please don't choose an ER by how busy it looks.**

- A child who is very drowsy or hard to wake is one of the situations where you should call 911 or go to the nearest ER now, without waiting.
- The nearest ER is the right one, children's hospital or not: every ER treats children. ERs see patients by how urgent their condition is, not by arrival order, so the most serious cases go first wherever they arrive.
- If you can't tell how serious it is, Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, day and night) can advise, but with a very drowsy child, don't let a call delay going.

For context only, not as a reason to drive farther or wait, here is what MSSS reports for Montréal's two children's ERs:

- CHU Sainte-Justine: 125% stretcher occupancy, 39 children waiting to see a physician.
- Montreal Children's: 175% stretcher occupancy, 27 children waiting to see a physician.

These counts can't tell which ER would see your child sooner. They don't show staffing or triage priorities, they change from hour to hour, and MSSS itself warns that a lower count doesn't mean being seen faster.
