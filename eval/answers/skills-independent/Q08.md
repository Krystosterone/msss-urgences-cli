**Orthopedic surgery waitlists are shorter than two years ago, in Montréal and across Québec, and the share of people waiting over 6 months has dropped. The latest period ticked up slightly, which is too short to call a trend.**

I looked at four fiscal periods: period 4 of each of the last three fiscal years, plus the period just before the latest one.

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery**

| Period | People waiting | Over 6 months | Over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 8,437 | 2,914 (35%) | 685 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 7,810 | 2,918 (37%) | 463 (6%) |
| 2627-P03 | 6,052 | 1,824 (30%) | 218 (4%) |
| 2627-P04 | 6,265 | 1,891 (30%) | 226 (4%) |

**Québec (sum of the regions reported), orthopedic surgery**

| Period | People waiting | Over 6 months | Over 12 months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2425-P04 | 38,763 | 13,640 (35%) | 3,193 (8%) |
| 2526-P04 | 34,513 | 11,613 (34%) | 2,085 (6%) |
| 2627-P03 | 28,530 | 7,207 (25%) | 969 (3%) |
| 2627-P04 | 29,359 | 7,900 (27%) | 968 (3%) |

- **Montréal:** the share waiting over 6 months fell from 35% to 30%, and the number waiting over a year from 685 to 226. In the latest period (2627-P04), Montréal's share over 6 months (30%) is a bit above the provincial share (27%).
- **Latest period:** from 2627-P03 to 2627-P04 the Montréal list grew from 6,052 to 6,265 people, and those over 6 months from 1,824 to 1,891. One period (about four weeks) is noise, not a trend.
- **Province, with a caveat:** the 2026-2027 periods exclude Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported (`missingRegions`: RSS04). That region had 2,385 orthopedic patients waiting in 2425-P04 and 2,503 in 2526-P04, so part of the provincial drop in the count is just the missing region. The percentages are less sensitive to that, but they still leave it out.
- **Regions differ a lot:** in 2627-P04, Outaouais had 52% of its orthopedic patients waiting over 6 months, the highest share in the province.

**Limits:** each figure is a snapshot at the end of a fiscal period, not today. The wait is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate, so time spent getting a family doctor or a specialist appointment isn't included. These are regional totals, not per-hospital figures, and they can't predict any one person's wait; the surgeon's office is the authority on that. If you're worried about your health while waiting, contact your surgeon's office or Info-Santé 811.
