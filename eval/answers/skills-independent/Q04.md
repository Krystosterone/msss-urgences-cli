**Ways to actually get a non-urgent consultation**

- **A pharmacist** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe. Ask whether they can help with your ear pain.
- **Info-Santé 811** gives a nurse's advice by phone, day and night, and can tell you where to go.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor?** The Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811. Check Québec.ca for current eligibility.
- **To find an actual slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec or the clinic's own booking system.

If the pain changes or you're unsure how serious it is, call 811; if symptoms could be serious, go to the nearest ER or call 911.

**Limits:** these counts are appointments that clinics connected to the booking hub offered for each date. Some were booked, some are reserved for the clinic's own patients, and clinics outside the hub aren't counted. More offers means a clinic that offers more through the hub, not better odds for you.
