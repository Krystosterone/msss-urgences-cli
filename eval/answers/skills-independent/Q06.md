**No one can tell you which ER will see you fastest or how long you'll wait.** MSSS doesn't publish wait times, and its crowding counts can't predict one: ERs see people by triage priority, and the numbers don't show staffing or who arrives next.

- If you're unsure how serious the injury is, Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, day and night) can advise you, including on whether you need an ER at all. If it could be serious, go to the nearest ER or call 911.
- If it turns out not to need the ER, other routes exist: a pharmacist for advice, your own clinic or GMF, or a clinic appointment through Rendez-vous santé Québec (the GAP, at Québec.ca or through 811, if you have no family doctor).
