**No. Finding no clinic appointment tonight doesn't by itself mean you need the ER: whether you do depends on your symptoms, and that's a medical question the appointment data can't answer.**

- **If your symptoms could be serious** (trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, being very drowsy or hard to wake, thoughts of suicide), call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room now, whatever the clinic listings show.
- **If you're not sure how serious it is,** call Info-Santé 811: a nurse answers 24/7 and can tell you whether to go to the ER tonight, see a clinic tomorrow, or manage at home for now.
- **Other routes:** a pharmacist can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe; your own family doctor or GMF, if you have one; without a family doctor, the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), at Québec.ca or through 811. Rendez-vous santé Québec or a clinic's own booking system shows real slots as they open up.

Some context on what you saw: late-night clinic appointments are rare in your area. In the RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord, over 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28 (the latest week MSSS has published), only one clinic, District Médical, offered physician appointments scheduled between 21h and 6h: 25 in the whole week, spread over 7 of 7 days. No clinic offered nurse appointments in that band. Evening slots (18h to 21h) were more common: 9 clinics offered some physician appointments in that band during the week. These are past offers, not openings tonight, and clinics outside the provincial booking hub aren't counted, so the data can't say what's free now.
