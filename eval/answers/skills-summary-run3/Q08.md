**Orthopedic waitlists have shrunk over the last three years, in Montréal and across Québec, and fewer people are waiting a long time. In Montréal, 30% of the 6,265 people waiting for orthopedic surgery had waited over 6 months in period 2627-P04, down from 37% a year earlier.**

Same period (period 4, which ends in late July) in each of the last three fiscal years, so the windows are comparable:

**Montréal (orthopedic surgery)**

- 2627-P04: 6,265 waiting; 1,891 over 6 months (30%), 226 over a year (4%).
- 2526-P04: 7,810 waiting; 2,918 over 6 months (37%), 463 over a year (6%).
- 2425-P04: 8,437 waiting; 2,914 over 6 months (35%), 685 over a year (8%).

**Québec as a whole (orthopedic surgery)**

- 2627-P04: 29,359 waiting; 7,900 over 6 months (27%), 968 over a year (3%).
- 2526-P04: 34,513 waiting; 11,613 over 6 months (34%), 2,085 over a year (6%).
- 2425-P04: 38,763 waiting; 13,640 over 6 months (35%), 3,193 over a year (8%).

**Read with care:**

- The 2627-P04 provincial figure **excludes Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec**, which isn't reported this period; it was included in both earlier years (2,503 orthopedic patients in 2526-P04). Part of the provincial drop in counts reflects that gap, so the percentages are the fairer comparison: 35% → 34% → 27% waiting over 6 months.
- Montréal's share over 6 months is a bit above the provincial share (30% vs 27%). Shares vary widely by region: Outaouais had the highest, 52%.
- From one period to the next the picture is mixed: Montréal's orthopedic list grew by 213 between 2627-P03 and 2627-P04, after falling by 296 the period before. One period (about four weeks) is noise; the three-year comparison is the more telling one.

**Limits:** these are end-of-period snapshots, and the latest (2627-P04, fiscal 2026-2027) ends in late July, so it is more than three months old. The wait is counted from the surgeon's decision to operate; time spent getting to a family doctor or a specialist isn't included. These are regional totals, not per hospital, and they don't predict anyone's own wait: their surgeon's office knows that best. If you're worried about your health while waiting, contact your surgeon's office, or call Info-Santé 811.

_Source: MSSS "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" (Chirurgie_ListeAttente.csv), periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04, 2627-P03 and 2627-P04._
