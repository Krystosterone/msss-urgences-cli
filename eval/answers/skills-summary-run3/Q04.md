**For non-urgent ear pain you have several routes, and around Ahuntsic 15 clinics regularly offer appointments through the provincial booking system, several of them every day and some in the evening.**

Ways to get a consultation:

- **Info-Santé 811:** a nurse's advice by phone, 24/7, including whether and where you should be seen.
- **A pharmacist:** can advise on minor problems and, for some conditions, prescribe.
- **Your own family doctor or GMF**, if you have one.
- **No family doctor:** the Guichet d'accès à la première ligne (GAP), online at Québec.ca or through 811 (check Québec.ca for current eligibility).
- **To book an actual slot:** Rendez-vous santé Québec, or the clinic's own booking system.

Which clinics usually offer appointments (RLS d'Ahuntsic – Montréal-Nord, 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28, data for all 7 of 7 days):

- 15 clinics offered physician appointments, and 9 offered appointments with a nurse.
- 5 clinics offered physician appointments on every one of those days: Centre médical Mieux-Être succ. Ahuntsic, CLSC Ahuntsic, Clinique médicale Omnisanté, District Médical and District Médical Junior.
- 9 clinics offered at least some physician appointments scheduled between 18h and 21h, for example Clinique médicale Désy (on 4 of 7 days) and CLSC de Montréal-Nord (on 5 of 7 days); District Médical did on 7 of 7 days.
- The network also covers Montréal-Nord, so check where each clinic actually is; a clinic in a neighbouring network may be closer to you.

**What this can't tell you:** these are appointments that clinics on the provincial booking hub *offered* for past dates, not openings now. Some were booked or reserved for the clinic's own patients, and clinics outside the hub aren't counted. MSSS republishes the file weekly (Thursdays at 14h), so the newest date is already 8 days old.

Since you describe it as not urgent, these routes fit. Call 811 or go to an ER if things change: a high fever, severe or worsening pain, swelling or redness behind the ear, a stiff neck, or feeling very unwell.

_Source: MSSS "Rendez-vous en clinique" file (published weekly), data for 2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28._
