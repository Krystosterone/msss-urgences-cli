That mean mixes adult and pediatric ERs; the city's other pediatric ER, the Montreal Children's, is at 183%.

That's normal day-to-day variation. Walk-in (ambulatory) stays were 4.3 h against 4.1 h, +3%, also normal.
- **Against the same hour in the snapshot archive:** in the latest archived snapshot (the same 08:45 file) it was at 87.5%, against 68.8% typical for this hour (+18.7 points). But the archive only starts 2026-11-03 at 22:45 (35 snapshots in all, only 2 snapshots at this hour, today's included), so that "typical" rests on one other morning, and there is no same-weekday comparison at all. Too thin to call this busier or quieter than usual.

My reading: nothing in the data suggests unusual crowding right now. These are head counts, not wait times: children are seen by triage priority, and MSSS warns that a low count doesn't mean being seen sooner. For the official estimated wait for a non-priority case, see Québec.ca's "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences).
