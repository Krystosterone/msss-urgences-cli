**The data can't tell which ER will see you fastest, and it contains no wait time. For a sprain you may not need the ER at all.**

**How long you'd wait:** the MSSS open data has no wait time, and occupancy or head counts can't be turned into one. Québec.ca's "Situation dans les urgences" page (quebec.ca/SituationUrgences) shows, for each ER, an estimated wait to see a physician for a non-priority case. It's an estimate that depends on the staff and patients present when you arrive, doesn't apply to urgent cases, and isn't a promise.

**Other routes for a sprain:** a pharmacist can advise you (and, for some conditions, prescribe); Info-Santé 811 gives a nurse's advice 24/7; your own family doctor or GMF; without a family doctor, the GAP (Guichet d'accès à la première ligne, at Québec.ca or through 811); and Rendez-vous santé Québec or a clinic's booking system for an appointment.

**Go to an ER (or call 911) instead** if you can't put any weight on the foot, the ankle looks deformed, or the foot is numb, cold or pale.
