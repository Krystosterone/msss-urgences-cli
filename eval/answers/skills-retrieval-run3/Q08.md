**Orthopedic waitlists have improved over the last three years, in Montréal and across Québec: fewer people waiting, and a smaller share waiting over 6 months. Montréal's long-wait share is now a little above the provincial one.**

Same period (period 4, which ends in late July) in each of the last three fiscal years, so the windows are comparable:

**Montréal, orthopedic surgery**

- 2425-P04: 8,437 waiting · 2,914 over 6 months (35%) · 685 over a year (8%)
- 2526-P04: 7,810 waiting · 2,918 over 6 months (37%) · 463 over a year (6%)
- 2627-P04: 6,265 waiting · 1,891 over 6 months (30%) · 226 over a year (4%)

**Québec, orthopedic surgery (sum of the regions reported)**

- 2425-P04: 38,763 waiting · 13,640 over 6 months (35%) · 3,193 over a year (8%)
- 2526-P04: 34,513 waiting · 11,613 over 6 months (34%) · 2,085 over a year (6%)
- 2627-P04: 29,359 waiting · 7,900 over 6 months (27%) · 968 over a year (3%)

- **The long-wait share is the telling figure.** Montréal went from 35% to 37% over 6 months, then down to 30%; the province from 35% to 34%, then 27%. People waiting over a year fell in both.
- **Coverage:** the 2627-P04 provincial figure excludes Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, which isn't reported this period. It was included the two earlier years (2,503 waiting for orthopedic surgery in 2526-P04), so part of the provincial drop in counts reflects that missing region; the percentages are the safer comparison.
- **Latest period-to-period:** from 2627-P03 to 2627-P04, Montréal went from 6,052 to 6,265 waiting and the province from 28,530 to 29,359 (both periods exclude the same region). One period is noise, not a trend.

**Limits:** these are end-of-period snapshots, and the latest (2627-P04, fiscal 2026-2027) is now several months old. Wait time counts from the surgeon's decision to operate, not from the first visit or specialist referral. They are regional totals, not per hospital, and don't predict any one person's wait. If you're waiting and worried about your health, your surgeon's office is the authority, or call Info-Santé 811.

_MSSS "Chirurgies – Portrait de la liste d'attente" file; periods 2425-P04, 2526-P04 and 2627-P04, plus 2627-P03._
