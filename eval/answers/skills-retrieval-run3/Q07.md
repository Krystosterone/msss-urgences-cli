**Not steadily. Across the five fiscal years that can be compared, Montréal's ER strain rose to a peak in 2022-2023 and then eased, ending 2025-2026 close to its 2021-2022 level. This year's regional figures can't be added to the trend, because four Montréal ERs are missing from them.**

The MSSS cumulative ER file has no occupancy rate, so this uses its strain indicators: the mean stay on a stretcher, the share of stretcher visits lasting 24 h or 48 h or more, and the mean delay from triage to a first physician. Lower is better for all of them. Each year covers the same window, April 1 → period 7 (roughly mid-October).

**Montréal region, April to period 7:**

- 2021-2022 (21 ERs): stretcher stay 18.0 h · 25.2% of stretcher visits over 24 h · 6.7% over 48 h · delay to physician 2.6 h
- 2022-2023 (21 ERs): stretcher stay 19.9 h · 27.3% of stretcher visits over 24 h · 9.2% over 48 h · delay to physician 2.8 h
- 2023-2024 (21 ERs): stretcher stay 19.2 h · 26.7% of stretcher visits over 24 h · 7.8% over 48 h · delay to physician 2.7 h
- 2024-2025 (21 ERs): stretcher stay 18.7 h · 25.9% of stretcher visits over 24 h · 7.2% over 48 h · delay to physician 2.8 h
- 2025-2026 (21 ERs): stretcher stay 18.4 h · 24.6% of stretcher visits over 24 h · 6.7% over 48 h · delay to physician 2.6 h
- 2026-2027 (17 ERs): stretcher stay 19.0 h · 23.9% of stretcher visits over 24 h · 7.3% over 48 h · delay to physician 2.7 h *(not comparable)*

- **2021-2022 → 2022-2023:** clearly worse (longer stays, more 24 h+ stays, longer delay).
- **2022-2023 → 2025-2026:** a gradual improvement each year on stays and the 24 h share, back to about where 2021-2022 was. The delay to a physician moved less (within a few tenths of an hour).
- **2026-2027 so far:** the region's total covers 17 ERs against 21 every earlier year (change of -4), so its totals, means and shares describe a different set of ERs and aren't comparable. The four Montréal ERs with no row in this fiscal year's file in the live join are Hôpital du Sacré-Coeur-de-Montréal, Hôpital Fleury, Hôpital Jean-Talon and Hôpital en santé mentale Albert-Prévost; that is probably the same set, but the cumulative output doesn't name the facilities.
- **One ER with like-for-like data, as an example:** at Hôpital Maisonneuve-Rosemont, the share of stretcher visits over 24 h went from 32.9% in 2021-2022 to 28.5% in 2025-2026, and is 36.6% so far in 2026-2027, its highest of the six years. A single hospital isn't the region, but it shows this year can look worse in places.
- **Province, for context:** the provincial 24 h share was 20.9% in 2021-2022, 23.5% in 2022-2023 and 20.3% in 2025-2026, the same rise-then-ease shape. The 2026-2027 provincial total covers 108 ERs against 116 the year before and has no data for Mauricie et Centre-du-Québec, so it isn't comparable either.

Occupancy over time: the archive of hourly snapshots only starts 2026-11-03 at 22:45, days rather than years, so it can't show a trend. Right now, Montréal's rate is 161% (one snapshot, not a trend). These are averages over past visits and don't say how long a visit today would take.
