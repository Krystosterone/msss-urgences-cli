**A 2-year-old who is very drowsy with a high fever needs to be seen now: call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room right away. Please don't choose between ERs by how busy they are.**

- A child who is very drowsy or hard to wake is a warning sign that needs urgent assessment. Call 911 if your child is hard to wake, has trouble breathing, has a seizure, or seems to be getting worse.
- Any ER can assess and stabilize a child, not only the two pediatric ones; the nearest one is the right one. ERs see children by triage priority, so the most serious cases are seen first wherever they arrive.
- If at any point you're unsure what to do, Info-Santé 811 (a nurse, 24/7) can advise you, but with a very drowsy child, don't delay going in to make a call.

The data can't tell you which ER would see your child faster. For context only, not as a reason to drive farther: at this morning's snapshot, CHU Sainte-Justine was at 88% stretcher occupancy with 9 children waiting to see a physician, and the Montreal Children's at 183% with 21 waiting. These are head counts at one moment, not wait times, and they can change quickly.
