**No. Finding no clinic appointment after 9 pm doesn't mean you need the ER, and it doesn't mean you don't. That depends on your symptoms, not on the appointment listings.**

- **If your symptoms could be serious** (trouble breathing, chest pain, signs of a stroke, heavy bleeding, a seizure, someone very drowsy or hard to wake, thoughts of suicide), call 911 or go to the nearest ER now. Don't wait for a clinic.
- **If you're not sure how serious it is,** call Info-Santé 811: a nurse, 24/7, who can tell you whether it can wait until a clinic appointment and where to go. Option 2 is Info-Social, for psychosocial needs.
- **For a minor problem:** a pharmacist can advise you and, for some conditions, prescribe. Other routes are your own family doctor or GMF; the GAP (Guichet d'accès à la première ligne, at Québec.ca or through 811) if you have no family doctor; and Rendez-vous santé Québec or a clinic's own booking system for the next available slot.

**Why an empty late-evening listing says little:** in MSSS's clinic file for the RLS d'Ahuntsic - Montréal-Nord (2026-10-22 to 2026-10-28), appointments scheduled after 9 pm were rare. District Médical was the only clinic with any in the 21h–6h band: 2 on Thursday 2026-10-22, 3 on 2026-10-28, and at most 6 on any day (2026-10-24). Early-evening appointments (18h–21h) were more common, for example 25 at District Médical on 2026-10-28. These are past offers, not what's open tonight; the file is published weekly and only covers clinics on the provincial booking hub.
